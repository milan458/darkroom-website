Privacy policy

Website and beta sign-up · Last updated 21 October 2026

This page covers darkroom.buzz and the beta sign-up form on it. The Darkroom app has its own privacy policy: Darkroom app privacy policy.

Who we are

Darkroom is made by Inertia Projects. You can reach us at business@inertiaprojects.info.

If you join the beta

When you enter your email address and press “Get the beta”, the page shows you the TestFlight link and we receive your email address and the time you signed up. They are stored in a Google Sheet that only we can open, sent there through Google Apps Script.

We use your email address only to contact you about the Darkroom beta. We don’t sell it, and we don’t share it with anyone else.

To have your email address removed, write to business@inertiaprojects.info from that address and we’ll delete it.

Just visiting

  • We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind, and we don’t set cookies.
  • Our fonts are served from darkroom.buzz itself, so loading this page doesn’t contact Google Fonts.
  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare handles your IP address and basic request details to deliver pages and protect the site from abuse.
  • Your browser remembers your light or dark choice and, if you joined the beta, the email you used, so the page can show that you’re on the list. This stays in your browser; “Use a different email” clears it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the date at the top.
