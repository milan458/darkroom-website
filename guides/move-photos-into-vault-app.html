How to move photos into a vault app on iPhone

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 26 November 2026

Short answer

Import the photos from inside the vault app, check they arrived in full, then delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted. Importing only copies a photo: until you delete the original, it's still in your library, in iCloud Photos, and on every device signed in to your Apple Account.

Moving photos into a vault looks like one step, but it's really three: copy them in, check them, and remove every other copy. Most people do the first and forget the rest, so the "hidden" photos are still in their camera roll, in Recently Deleted, or on their iPad. This guide covers all three, and the places stray copies hide. Before you start, make sure your vault keeps full resolution, because you're about to delete the originals.

What should you check before you move photos?

Make sure the vault is one you'll keep. Once the originals are gone, the vault holds your only copy on the phone.

  • Encryption. The vault should encrypt photos on the iPhone, not just hide them behind a PIN. See are photo vault apps safe?
  • A backup. Many vaults keep photos only on the phone, so deleting the app deletes them. Check how yours backs up first.
  • Quality. Test one photo: import it, export it, and compare size, resolution and date.
  • A way out. Check that it can export everything again, on every plan.
  • Its limit. Free plans often cap the number of photos or videos.

Does the vault need access to all your photos?

No. Since iOS 14, apps can use Apple's photo picker, which hands over only the photos you select. You can also give an app Limited Access to chosen photos, or Full Access to your whole library.

Check or change it in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos, then the app. A vault needs Full Access only if it offers to delete originals for you or to scan your library; otherwise the picker is enough.

How do you import photos into a vault?

From inside the vault app, in batches you can check. The buttons have different names, but the steps are the same:

  1. Open the vault and the album you want the photos in.
  2. Tap the add or import button and choose Photos (or Files, for photos saved there).
  3. Select the photos and videos. Start with one album or one day, not your whole library.
  4. Wait until it finishes. Large videos and photos stored in iCloud take longer, because iOS downloads the original first. Keep the app open and stay on Wi-Fi.
  5. Repeat for the next batch.

Some vaults also add photos from the share sheet in Photos, or have their own camera, whose photos never reach your camera roll. Obscura's App Store listing, for example, says it can "import in batches and delete the originals from Photos".

How do you check the photos arrived?

Compare counts, open a few photos, and play the videos. Before you delete anything:

  1. Count. The number in the vault should match what you selected.
  2. Open a few full-size photos, including old ones, not just thumbnails.
  3. Play videos to the end, and press on Live Photos to see them move.
  4. Check the backup has finished, if your vault has one.

How do you delete the originals?

Delete them in Photos, then empty Recently Deleted. Otherwise iOS keeps them for 30 days.

  1. Select the same photos in Photos. Searching by date or opening the same album makes this easier.
  2. Tap the Delete button and confirm. With iCloud Photos on, iOS warns that they'll be deleted from all your devices. That's what you want here, but only after checking the vault.
  3. Open Recently Deleted under Utilities in Photos, and unlock it with Face ID or your passcode.
  4. Tap Select, then Delete All (or delete just the photos you moved) and confirm.
Watch out

Deleting from Recently Deleted can't be undone. Do it only after the vault holds every photo, and its backup, if it has one, has finished.

Where else can copies of the photos be?

In more places than your camera roll. Check each one you use:

  • The Hidden album. Photos you hid earlier aren't in your main library view, but they're still there.
  • Shared albums. Everyone you shared an album with can still see it, and may have saved copies. Delete the photos from the shared album too.
  • The iCloud Shared Photo Library. Its photos belong to everyone in it. Check which library the photos are in before you delete, and remember others may have saved copies.
  • Messages, WhatsApp and other chats. Photos you sent or received stay in the conversation. WhatsApp can also save incoming photos to your library by itself: turn off Save to Photos in its chat settings.
  • Other photo backups, such as Google Photos, OneDrive or Dropbox camera uploads.
  • Your Mac or PC, in its Photos library or in an iPhone backup on that computer.

Hidden photos and how they sync are explained in how to hide photos on iPhone.

How do you move photos from another vault app?

Export from the old vault, import into the new one, check, and only then delete the old app. Vault apps can't read each other's files, so your camera roll or Files is the bridge.

  1. Export everything from the old vault to Photos or Files, album by album. Our guide to exporting photos from a vault app lists how popular apps do it.
  2. Check the exports: counts, full size, dates and videos.
  3. Import them into the new vault, using the steps above.
  4. Check the new vault, and let its backup finish.
  5. Delete the exported copies from Photos or Files, and empty Recently Deleted.
  6. Cancel the old subscription, then delete the old app. Deleting an app removes everything stored only inside it; see does deleting a vault app delete your photos?

Do it in one sitting if you can. While the photos sit in your camera roll between the two vaults, they're unprotected.

Questions people ask

Does moving photos to a vault delete them from iCloud?

No. Importing copies them. They leave iCloud Photos only when you delete them in Photos and empty Recently Deleted.

Will the photos still be in Recently Deleted?

Yes, for 30 days after you delete them in Photos. Empty Recently Deleted to remove them for good.

Can I move photos without giving the app my whole library?

Yes. Apple's photo picker gives the app only the photos you choose, and you can set Limited Access in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.

What happens to the photos if I delete the vault app?

Unless the app backed them up, they're deleted with it. Export them, or check the app's backup, before you delete it.
