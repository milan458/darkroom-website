Vaultaire, Obscura and Encamera say in their listings that they have no ads, and so does Noxium, which we make. These apps show ads or sell their removal, from each developer's own App Store page:

  • Calculator#: free with ads; the $4.99 yearly purchase removes them for a year.
  • Privault: sells "Remove ADs" for $2.99 (see our Privault review).

Ads matter more than annoyance here. Keepsafe, Privault, KYMS, PV and SPV all list Advertising Data in their privacy labels, and all of them list some data under "Data Used to Track You". In our full comparison, 9 of 17 vault apps from other developers list data used to track you.

What do free photo vault plans include?

Free plans cap different things, and some leave out backup. From each app's listing or help pages:

  • Noxium: 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes, 1 decoy vault, and encrypted iCloud backup and restore.
  • Vaultaire: up to 3 vaults, 100 photos and 10 videos in total, with encrypted iCloud backup on by default.
  • Obscura: up to 50 photos and documents, with a free encrypted export of the whole vault.
  • Keepsafe: the free version limits its private cloud to 200 items (Keepsafe Help Center), and shows ads.
  • Private Photo Vault: its listing says the free version doesn't back up to its Cloud Vault.
  • Apple's Hidden album: free and built into Photos, locked with Face ID, Touch ID or a passcode (Apple Support). It isn't a separate vault: anyone who knows your iPhone passcode can open it.

Check whether backup is in the free plan. Without it, a free user's photos may exist on one phone only, and losing that phone loses them. Our guide on moving a photo vault to a new iPhone covers what a backup has to do to survive a phone change.

What happens to your photos if you stop paying?

Check this before anything else, because a vault holds your own photos. If a music subscription lapses, you lose songs you can stream elsewhere. If a vault limits access when you stop paying, your photos are on the other side of the paywall.

The good answer is: you keep full access to everything already in the vault, including export and backup, and only new imports or premium features stop. The bad answer is anything that limits viewing, exporting or restoring photos you already have. An App Store listing may not say, so check the app's help pages or ask the developer before you move a library in.

Noxium's rule, which we make: if Pro ends, you keep full access to view, export and back up everything already in your vault. Only adding past the free limits and Pro features (unlimited items, emergency erase, iPhone and iPad sync, custom icons, album patterns, shared vaults) pause. What Noxium doesn't protect: photos still in your Photos library before you import them, and copies you've already sent. And nobody can reset a forgotten pattern, so keep the recovery phrase you write down at setup.

How do you choose a vault you won't regret paying for?

  1. Start free with a few dozen photos and a couple of videos. Test import, playback, export and backup before you pay.
  2. Find the "stop paying" rule in the app or its support pages.
  3. Compare a year of subscription with the one-time price. If you'll use it for years, one-time usually wins.
  4. Check what one-time covers: cloud storage, updates and family members can be sold separately.
  5. Check the privacy label on the App Store page for "Data Used to Track You".
  6. Check the decoy if someone might make you open the app. See what a decoy vault is and what it can't do.

Questions people ask

Is there a photo vault app with no subscription?

Yes. Vaultaire, Obscura and Noxium sell lifetime Pro for $39.99, and PV, Privault, MaxVault, Encamera and Calculator Lock list one-time purchases too, on their US App Store pages checked 28 November 2026. Apple's Hidden album is free.

Which photo vault app has no ads?

Vaultaire, Obscura, Encamera and Noxium say they have no ads. Keepsafe's free version, Calculator#, Privault, KYMS, PV and Calculator Lock show ads or sell ad removal. Our Calculator# review covers that app in detail.

Does Keepsafe have a lifetime plan?

Yes, its help center lists a Lifetime plan beside Monthly and Yearly, with a cloud limit of 100,000 items. Neither the help center nor the US App Store page shows its price, so check the purchase screen in the app.

What is a good Keepsafe alternative without a subscription?

Vaultaire, Obscura and Noxium, which we make, each sell lifetime Pro for $39.99, need no account and say they have no ads. Encamera sells a $99.99 Lifetime and needs no account either. See Keepsafe alternatives.

Will I lose my photos if I cancel a vault subscription?

It depends on the app. Check its help pages or ask the developer before you subscribe. In Noxium you keep full access to view, export and back up everything already in your vault.

Is a lifetime vault purchase worth it?

Usually, if you'll use the vault for more than about two years. The lifetime prices here equal about one to three years of the yearly plan. Try the free plan first to make sure the app works for you.
