Vaultaire vs Keepsafe: which photo vault fits you?

By Inertia Projects·Published 26 October 2026·Last updated 28 November 2026

Short answer

The main difference is who holds your photos. Vaultaire needs no account and backs up, encrypted, to your own iCloud, including on its free plan. Keepsafe signs you in with an email address and keeps its cloud copy on Keepsafe's own servers. On the US App Store (checked 28 November 2026), Vaultaire Pro is $19.99 a year or $39.99 once; Keepsafe Premium is $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year. Keepsafe is far better known, with about 387,000 ratings against Vaultaire's 72.

Choose Vaultaire if you want no account and an encrypted backup in your own iCloud. Choose Keepsafe if you want an email account to sign in with and don't mind a copy of your vault living on Keepsafe's servers. This comparison is for iPhone users picking between the two, or thinking of leaving Keepsafe, and it uses each app's US App Store page and its maker's own pages, checked 28 November 2026.

Keepsafe is one of the best-known vaults on the iPhone, with about 387,000 US ratings averaging 4.7 (App Store). Vaultaire, from Wraxle LLC, is new, with 72 ratings averaging 4.7 (App Store). Both hide photos and videos behind a lock, but they're built on different ideas about accounts and the cloud.

How do Vaultaire and Keepsafe differ?

Vaultaire works without an account and keeps backups in your iCloud; Keepsafe works through an email account and its own cloud. Most other differences follow from that.

Do you need an account for Vaultaire or Keepsafe?

Vaultaire doesn't ask for one. Keepsafe does: you sign in with an email address.

Vaultaire's listing says "No account is required", and its website says no email, no phone number and no signup (vaultaire.app). Keepsafe sends an access code to your account email when you sign in on a new device. Its help center says that if you lose access to that email, the account and its contents can't be recovered, and suggests adding a second email address (Keepsafe Help Center).

So an account is not a full safety net either. With no account, nobody can reset your code for you, so check what a no-account vault offers if you forget your code. Our guide on forgotten vault passwords covers what to look for.

Where do Vaultaire and Keepsafe back up your photos?

Vaultaire backs up to your own iCloud, encrypted, on both its free and Pro plans. Keepsafe backs up to its own cloud.

  • Vaultaire: the listing says iCloud backup is on by default, and the vault is encrypted on the device before it goes to your private iCloud. Its website says you can turn the backup off. Syncing across several devices needs Pro.
  • Keepsafe: the free plan backs up your first 200 items. Premium raises that to 10,000 and the Lifetime plan to 100,000 (Keepsafe Help Center). Premium's Space Saver compresses photos on the phone and keeps the originals in Keepsafe's cloud.
Watch out

If you use Keepsafe's Space Saver, the full-size originals are in Keepsafe's cloud, not on your phone. If you ever leave Keepsafe, export the originals, not the small copies.

Which is cheaper, Vaultaire or Keepsafe?

Vaultaire, on every plan we could compare: $19.99 a year against Keepsafe's $23.99, and $5.99 a month against $9.99. Vaultaire also sells Pro Lifetime for $39.99.

Vaultaire's full US price list: Pro Weekly $1.99, Pro Monthly $5.99, Pro Annual $19.99, Pro Lifetime $39.99, and Family plans at $49.99 a year or $99.99 once (App Store). Keepsafe's listing shows Monthly Premium at $9.99 and Yearly Premium at $23.99, plus other Premium items from $4.99 to $35.99 that it doesn't label (App Store). Keepsafe's help center offers Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime plans but gives no prices.

Prices differ by country and change; check the App Store in your region before you buy. More apps with one-time prices are in vaults without subscriptions or ads.

Which one is more secure?

Both say they encrypt your files. Vaultaire names AES-256-GCM in its listing; Keepsafe's help center says 256-bit AES, with all files encrypted at rest (Keepsafe Help Center).

Vaultaire's website goes further. It says AES-256-GCM covers file content, metadata and thumbnails, and that the key comes from your pattern through PBKDF2 (vaultaire.app). It also says plainly what that can't hide: a forensic examiner could still estimate how many vaults exist.

The bigger difference is where copies live. With Vaultaire, the copies are on your devices and in your iCloud. With Keepsafe, a copy also lives on Keepsafe's servers, tied to your account. For what the encryption terms mean, see how encrypted photo vaults work.

Does Vaultaire or Keepsafe have a decoy vault?

Both do. In Vaultaire, a different pattern opens a different vault, and Pro adds a duress vault. In Keepsafe, Premium lets you create a fake PIN that opens a decoy vault (Keepsafe Help Center).

Vaultaire's duress pattern opens one chosen vault and removes the other vaults from the device. Keepsafe's decoy needs a paid plan; Vaultaire's separate vaults work on the free plan, which allows up to 3. A decoy only helps if it looks real. Our guide on what a decoy vault is explains how they work and their limits.

Should you choose Vaultaire or Keepsafe?

Choose Vaultaire if you don't want an account or a company cloud. Choose Keepsafe if you want an email account to sign in with and a long track record, and don't mind its cloud or its ads on the free plan.

  • Vaultaire fits if you want no account, an encrypted backup in your own iCloud, a decoy on the free plan, and a one-time price.
  • Keepsafe fits if you want an email account and a backup that doesn't depend on your iCloud, and you're comfortable with Keepsafe storing your cloud copy and with its prices.

Moving from Keepsafe? Follow the steps in how to switch from Keepsafe without losing photos: export the originals, check them, import, and only then delete. Other no-account options are in our Keepsafe alternatives guide, and our Keepsafe review looks at Keepsafe on its own.

Questions people ask

Is Vaultaire better than Keepsafe?

It depends on what you want. Vaultaire needs no account, backs up to your own iCloud and costs less on the US App Store. Keepsafe gives you an email account to sign in with and a much longer track record, with its cloud copy on Keepsafe's servers.

Are Vaultaire's reviews good?

So far, yes, but there are few of them. On 28 November 2026 its US App Store page showed 72 ratings averaging 4.7, against about 387,000 for Keepsafe. Our Vaultaire review looks at what the app does from its listing and website.

Is vaultaire.app the official Vaultaire website?

It appears to be. The site names Wraxle LLC as the maker, the same developer as Vaultaire's App Store listing, and describes the same app: no account, a 5×5 pattern and encrypted iCloud backup.

Does Vaultaire need an account?

No. Vaultaire works without an account, email address or phone number. Its backups go to your own iCloud, encrypted.

Is Keepsafe free?

Keepsafe has a free, ad-supported plan that backs up your first 200 items to its cloud. Premium costs $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year on the US App Store, checked 28 November 2026.

Can I move my photos from Keepsafe to Vaultaire?

Yes. Export them from Keepsafe to your Photos library at full quality, import them into Vaultaire, check the backup has finished, and only then delete them from Keepsafe.
