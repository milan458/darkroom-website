How to export photos from a vault app on iPhone

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 24 November 2026

Short answer

Open the vault, select the photos, and use its Export, Share or Save option to send them to Photos or Files. Then check the count, that videos play, and that photos are full size before you delete anything. Exported copies are no longer protected by the vault, so move them on quickly.

You might want photos out of a vault to switch apps, to back them up, to print them, or before you delete the app. Every vault does it a little differently, and some quietly lose quality or dates on the way out. This guide gives the general steps, the routes each popular app offers, and the checks that tell you nothing was lost. Switching to a new phone instead? See moving a photo vault to a new iPhone.

How do you export photos from a vault app?

Select, export, check. The buttons have different names in each app, but the steps are the same:

  1. Open the vault and the album you want.
  2. Tap Select (or touch and hold) and choose the photos and videos. Many apps have a Select All.
  3. Tap Export, Share or the share icon, and choose where they go: the Photos library, Files, or a computer.
  4. Wait for it to finish. Large videos can take minutes. Keep the app open.
  5. Check the copies where they landed, before you delete anything from the vault.

Do albums one at a time if the vault has many. It's easier to check counts album by album.

How do popular vault apps export?

From their App Store listings and our reviews:

  • Keepsafe: its help center says to open an album, select items, tap the send icon and choose Export to Camera Roll. If you use Space Saver, check exports are full size.
  • Private Photo Vault and Vaultaire: their listings say you can export back to the Photos app.
  • SPV: its listing tells you to export photos and videos to the photo gallery before deleting the app.
  • Secret Photo Vault, Safe Lock and PV: Wi-Fi transfer to a computer; Secret Photo Vault and Safe Lock also export to the photo library, and Safe Lock to Dropbox, Google Drive and others.
  • Obscura: share items out, or export the whole vault as one encrypted .obscura file you can restore later.

Menu names change with app updates. If you can't find export, check the app's help pages or contact the developer before deleting anything.

How do you check nothing was lost?

Compare counts, play videos, and look at size and date.

  1. Count. The number of exported items should match the album in the vault.
  2. Play videos to the end. A video that stops early didn't export fully.
  3. Check size. Open a photo, swipe up and look at its resolution and file size. Much smaller than the camera's usual size means the vault exported a compressed copy. See does a photo vault keep full resolution?
  4. Check dates. Photos should land on the day they were taken, not today. If they're all dated today, the vault didn't keep the original date.
  5. Check Live Photos still move when you press on them.
Watch out

Exported copies are ordinary files. Anyone with your passcode can see photos in your library, and they upload to iCloud Photos if it's on. Move them into their new home in the same sitting.

What should you do after exporting?

Move the copies to where they'll live, then tidy up both ends.

  • Moving to another vault: import from Photos, check the new vault's count, then delete the exported copies from Photos and empty Recently Deleted, or they stay there for 30 days.
  • Keeping them on a computer: copy them to an encrypted drive or folder, then delete them from the phone's Photos or Files.
  • Leaving the old vault: keep it until you've checked everything in the new place, then delete its items and cancel any subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Our guide to deleting a vault app covers the safe order.

Questions people ask

How do I get photos out of a vault app?

Open the vault, select the photos, and tap Export, Share or Save to send them to Photos or Files. Check them before you delete anything.

Will exporting reduce photo quality?

It depends on the app. Some keep originals; others saved a compressed copy when you imported. Compare file sizes after exporting.

Can I export everything at once?

Many apps have Select All in each album. Obscura exports the whole vault as one encrypted file. Doing it album by album makes checking easier.

Can I export if my subscription ended?

Usually, but check first. Noxium and Obscura say export keeps working after a subscription ends.
