How to hide photos from Spotlight search on iPhone

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 24 November 2026

Short answer

Turn off Show Content in Search for Photos. It's in the Photos app's Siri & Search (or Search) settings, reached from Settings › Apps › Photos on iOS 18 and later. Photos then stops showing individual pictures when you swipe down to search. To keep one photo out of everything, hide it as well.

Swipe down on the Home Screen, type a word like "beach" or a friend's name, and Spotlight can show matching photos from your library before you've opened Photos. That's handy until someone else is holding your phone. This guide shows how to stop it, what each switch does, and where else photos can surface. For hiding photos in general, see how to hide photos on iPhone.

Why do my photos show up in Spotlight search?

Because Photos shares its content with search by default. Your iPhone recognises people, places, objects and text in your pictures, and Spotlight uses that to show matches from your library.

This all happens on the iPhone; it isn't a web search. But anyone who can unlock your phone and swipe down can see the results.

How do you stop photos appearing in Spotlight?

Turn off Show Content in Search for the Photos app. On iOS 26:

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Photos.
  2. Tap Siri & Search (on some versions it's split into Siri and Search; open Search).
  3. Turn off Show Content in Search. Individual photos stop appearing in Spotlight results.
  4. Optionally turn off Show App in Search too. Then the Photos app itself won't be suggested either.
  5. Swipe down on the Home Screen and search for a word that used to bring up photos, to check.

On iOS 17 and earlier the same switches are under Settings › Siri & Search › Photos. Turning them off doesn't delete or move any photo; it only changes what Spotlight shows.

What about suggestions on the Home Screen?

Turn those off on the same screen. Look for Show on Home Screen, Suggest App and Show When Sharing, and switch off the ones you don't want. They control whether Photos appears in Siri Suggestions, the App Library and the share sheet.

The Photos widget is separate: it shows featured photos on your Home Screen. Touch and hold it and tap Remove Widget if you don't want any photos showing there.

How do you hide just one photo from search?

Hide it in Photos. A hidden photo moves to the Hidden album and, Apple says, doesn't appear anywhere else in Photos, including your Library, albums and the Photos widget.

  1. Touch and hold the photo in Photos.
  2. Tap Hide and confirm.
  3. Search for it in Spotlight to check it no longer appears.

The Hidden album is locked with Face ID or your passcode, and you can find it under Collections › Utilities. See how to view hidden photos on iPhone.

Does this stop search inside the Photos app?

No. These settings only affect Spotlight and suggestions. Search inside Photos still finds your photos by person, place and object, because it's part of the app.

Anyone who can open Photos can still search your library there. If that's the worry, hide the photos, or lock the Photos app: touch and hold its icon and tap Require Face ID (iOS 18 and later). Both fall back to your iPhone passcode; is the iPhone Hidden album private? explains what that means.

Where else can photos show up?

A few places don't follow the Spotlight switches.

Where iPhone photos can surface on their own, and how to stop it
PlaceHow to stop it
Spotlight searchTurn off Show Content in Search for Photos

Lock Screen Photo ShuffleChoose the photos yourself when you edit the wallpaper
Search inside PhotosHide the photo, or lock the Photos app
Your Mac and iPadSame switches on each device; hidden photos stay hidden everywhere with iCloud Photos

On a Mac, photos can appear in Spotlight too. Remove Photos from search results in System Settings › Spotlight.

Keep them outside your Photos library. Anything in the library, hidden or not, is indexed by your iPhone, and anyone with your passcode can open the Hidden album.

Questions people ask

How do I stop photos showing in iPhone search?

In the Photos app's Siri & Search settings, turn off Show Content in Search. On iOS 18 and later, start from Settings › Apps › Photos.

Do hidden photos show in Spotlight?

Hidden photos are kept out of your Library, albums and widgets. To be sure nothing from Photos appears in Spotlight, also turn off Show Content in Search.

Does turning off Show Content in Search delete photos?

No. It only changes what Spotlight shows. Your photos stay in your library and in iCloud Photos.

Can I still search inside the Photos app?

Yes. The Spotlight switches don't affect search inside Photos.
