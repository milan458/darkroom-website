Hidden photos disappeared on iPhone? How to find them

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 23 November 2026

Short answer

Usually they haven't gone anywhere. Check that Show Hidden Album is on in Settings › Apps › Photos, then look under Collections › Utilities › Hidden. If the album is empty, check Recently Deleted (items stay 30 days), make sure iCloud Photos is on with the right Apple Account, and look on your other devices.

Hidden photos seem to vanish for a handful of reasons, and most of them are settings, not data loss. Work through the checks below in order; the first two solve most cases. This guide is for iOS 26. If you're not sure where the Hidden album should be, start with how to view hidden photos on iPhone.

Why did the Hidden album disappear?

Most often because Show Hidden Album is turned off, which removes the album from Photos without touching the photos. Turn it back on:

  1. Open Settings › Apps › Photos.
  2. Turn on Show Hidden Album.
  3. Open Photos, tap Collections and scroll down to Utilities. Hidden is listed there with Recently Deleted.

After an update, the album may simply be somewhere new. Since the iOS 18 Photos redesign it sits under Collections › Utilities, near the bottom of the list, not under Albums.

Why don't hidden photos show in my other albums?

Because that's what hiding does. Apple says hidden items don't appear anywhere else in Photos, including your Library, other albums and the Photos widget. A photo you hid from inside an album is still a member of that album, but it shows only in Hidden until you unhide it.

To put a photo back, open Hidden, touch and hold the photo, and tap Unhide. It returns to your Library and its albums.

Why is the Hidden album empty?

The photos were deleted, unhidden, or haven't synced yet. Check each:

  • Deleted. Open Collections › Utilities › Recently Deleted and unlock it. Deleted photos stay there for 30 days. Select them and tap Recover.
  • Unhidden. Search your Library for the photos by date or place. If they're there, they were unhidden on this or another device.
  • Not synced yet. On a new or restored iPhone, iCloud Photos can take hours to download a large library. Leave the iPhone on Wi-Fi and power.
  • Deleted on another device. With iCloud Photos on, deleting a photo on your iPad or Mac deletes it everywhere. Check Recently Deleted on that device too.

Could iCloud be the cause?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is off, paused or signed in to a different Apple Account. Hidden photos sync only through iCloud Photos.

  1. Check the account at the top of Settings. It must be the Apple Account you used when you hid the photos.
  2. Check iCloud Photos in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos: Sync this iPhone should be on.
  3. Check storage. If iCloud is full, new photos stop uploading, so photos hidden on one device may never reach another.
  4. Check iCloud.com. Sign in at icloud.com/photos, click Hidden, then Show Hidden Photos. If they're there, they're safe in iCloud and your iPhone just hasn't synced.

If iCloud Photos was off, hidden photos are only in your iPhone's own library and its iCloud Backup. After a reset, they come back only when you restore that backup.

Can Screen Time hide the Hidden album?

Screen Time doesn't have a Hidden album setting, but it can block Photos as a whole. If an App Limit or Downtime blocks Photos, you'll see a Screen Time screen instead of your library. On a child's or managed iPhone, the parent or organization controls these settings in Settings › Screen Time.

What about the iCloud Shared Photo Library?

Photos hidden in a shared library move to the Hidden album for every participant (Apple: Hide your photos and videos on iCloud.com). If someone else in the shared library unhid or deleted a photo, it changes for you too. Check Recently Deleted, and switch the library view to Both Libraries so you're not looking at only one.

How do you stop this happening again?

Keep a second copy somewhere only you control. The Hidden album shares your Photos library, so anything that removes photos from the library, on any of your devices, can remove hidden ones too.

Questions people ask

Where did my Hidden album go?

In iOS 26 it's under Collections › Utilities in Photos. If it's not there, turn on Show Hidden Album in Settings › Apps › Photos.

Why are my hidden photos not showing in albums?

Hidden photos appear only in the Hidden album. Unhide a photo to see it in your Library and other albums again.

Can I recover hidden photos I deleted?

Within 30 days, from Recently Deleted. After that, only from an older iCloud Backup or computer backup made before you deleted them.

Why do my hidden photos look blurry?

With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the full-size copy stays in iCloud until you open the photo. Give it a moment on Wi-Fi and it loads in full.

Do hidden photos disappear after an iOS update?

No. Updates don't delete hidden photos, but they can move the album. Look under Collections › Utilities.
