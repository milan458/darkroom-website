How to view hidden photos on a Mac

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 22 November 2026

Short answer

Open Photos on your Mac, choose View › Show Hidden Photo Album, then click Hidden under Utilities in the sidebar and unlock it with Touch ID or your login password. Photos you hid on your iPhone show up there only if iCloud Photos is on for both devices, with the same Apple Account.

On a Mac, the Hidden album is both hidden and locked by default, so it's easy to think your hidden photos didn't sync. This guide shows where the album is in Photos for Mac (macOS Ventura and later, checked against macOS Sequoia), why photos you hid on your iPhone might not be there, and how to hide, unhide and lock photos on the Mac. For the same steps on your phone, see how to view hidden photos on iPhone.

How do you see the Hidden album on a Mac?

Turn the album on from the View menu, then open it from the sidebar and unlock it. Apple's steps (Apple: Hide photos and videos from view on Mac):

  1. Open Photos and click Library in the sidebar.
  2. Choose View › Show Hidden Photo Album in the menu bar.
  3. Click Hidden in the sidebar, under Utilities.
  4. Click View Album and unlock it with Touch ID or your Mac login password.

The album locks again when you close Photos or your Mac sleeps. To hide the album from the sidebar again, choose View › Hide Hidden Photo Album. That only removes the album from view; the photos stay hidden in your library.

Why aren't my iPhone's hidden photos on my Mac?

Almost always because iCloud Photos isn't on for one of the two devices, or they use different Apple Accounts. Hidden photos sync only through iCloud Photos, and Apple says photos you hide on one device are hidden on your others when it's on.

  1. On the iPhone, open Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos and check that Sync this iPhone is on.
  2. On the Mac, open Photos › Settings › iCloud and check that iCloud Photos is ticked.
  3. Check the account. Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account, in System Settings › [your name] on the Mac.
  4. Give it time. A large library can take hours to sync the first time. Photos shows its progress at the bottom of the Library view.
  5. Turn on the album with View › Show Hidden Photo Album, as above.

Without iCloud Photos, there's no sync. You can unhide the photos on your iPhone and import them with a cable, or AirDrop them from the Hidden album on the iPhone to the Mac. Either way, the copies on the Mac are ordinary photos, not hidden ones, until you hide them again.

How do you hide or unhide photos on a Mac?

Select the photos and use the Image menu: Image › Hide to hide, and Image › Unhide inside the Hidden album to bring them back.

  • Hide: select one or more photos, then choose Image › Hide [number] Photos, or press Command-L. Confirm, and they move to the Hidden album.
  • Unhide: open the Hidden album, select the photos, then choose Image › Unhide [number] Photos.
  • Everywhere at once: with iCloud Photos on, a photo you hide or unhide on the Mac is hidden or unhidden on your iPhone and iPad too.

How do you lock or unlock the Hidden album on a Mac?

It's locked by default on macOS Ventura and later. You can turn the lock off in Photos settings, but you usually shouldn't.

  1. Open Photos › Settings and click General.
  2. Find Use Touch ID or password. When it's ticked, the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums need Touch ID or your login password.
  3. Leave it ticked on any Mac that someone else uses or can unlock.
Watch out

The lock is your Mac login password. Anyone who knows it, or can sign in to your user account, can open your hidden photos on that Mac, including the ones you hid on your iPhone.

Can you see hidden photos on a Mac without the Photos app?

Yes, in a browser at icloud.com/photos. Sign in, click Hidden in the sidebar, then Show Hidden Photos (Apple: Hide your photos and videos on iCloud.com). If you use Advanced Data Protection, first turn on Access iCloud Data on the Web in Settings › [your name] › iCloud on your iPhone. The same route works on any computer, as our guide to viewing hidden photos on Windows explains.

Who else can see your hidden photos on a Mac?

Anyone who can open your user account on that Mac and knows its login password. Other user accounts on the same Mac have their own Photos libraries and don't see yours.

That's the same limit the Hidden album has on iPhone: one shared code opens it. If a partner or family member knows your Mac password, the album keeps photos out of casual view and nothing more. Read is the iPhone Hidden album private? for the full picture.

Questions people ask

Where is the Hidden album on Mac?

In the Photos sidebar under Utilities, once you choose View › Show Hidden Photo Album. It's hidden from the sidebar by default.

How do I unlock the Hidden album on my Mac?

Click View Album, then use Touch ID or type your Mac login password. There's no separate password for the album.

Do hidden photos sync between iPhone and Mac?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is on for both and they use the same Apple Account. A photo hidden on one is hidden on the other.

Why is Show Hidden Photo Album greyed out?

Click Library in the sidebar first. The View menu option applies to the library, so select it before you choose the menu item.

Does hiding a photo on Mac delete it?

No. It stays in your library and in iCloud Photos, just out of view. Unhide it from the Hidden album at any time.
