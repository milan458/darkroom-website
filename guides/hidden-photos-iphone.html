Hidden photos on iPhone: how to find, view, add, lock and move them

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

To view hidden photos on iPhone, open Photos, tap Collections, scroll down to Utilities and tap Hidden, then tap View Album and unlock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. If Hidden isn't there, turn on Show Hidden Album in Settings › Apps › Photos. To add a photo, touch and hold it and tap Hide.

The Hidden album is the iPhone's built-in place for photos you don't want in your main library. It's also easy to lose track of: Apple moved it during the iOS 18 Photos redesign, it can be switched off entirely, and it behaves differently on a Mac, on iCloud.com and on a new iPhone. This guide answers each of those questions for iOS 26, with Apple's own steps and links to the Apple Support pages they come from.

How do you view hidden photos on iPhone?

Open the Hidden album under Utilities in Photos, then unlock it. In iOS 26 it takes four taps (Apple: Hide photos with the Hidden album):

  1. Open Photos and tap Collections. On iPad, tap the sidebar button first.
  2. Scroll down to Utilities and tap Hidden.
  3. Tap View Album.
  4. Unlock it with Face ID, Touch ID or your iPhone passcode.

The album locks again when you leave Photos. On iOS 15 and earlier, look for Hidden under Albums › Utilities or Other Albums instead; it isn't locked on those versions.

Why can't you find the Hidden album?

Usually because Show Hidden Album is turned off, which removes the album from Photos without touching the photos in it. Turn it back on:

  1. Open Settings and tap Apps › Photos.
  2. Turn on Show Hidden Album. It's on by default.
  3. Go back to Photos, open Collections and scroll to Utilities.

Two other common causes: you're looking at the top of Collections, and Utilities sits near the bottom of the list; or iCloud Photos is still syncing on a new device, so photos hidden elsewhere haven't arrived yet. Hidden photos also disappear from your other albums: Apple says hidden items "don't appear anywhere else in Photos, like the Library, other albums, or even the Photos widget on your Home Screen". If a photo is missing from an album, check Hidden before you assume it's gone.

Can you view hidden photos without Face ID?

Yes, with your iPhone passcode. Face ID and Touch ID are a shortcut; when they fail or you tap the fallback, the passcode unlocks the Hidden album. There is no separate password for it. That also means anyone who knows your passcode can open it, which is the main limit of the album (more below).

How do you add photos to the Hidden album?

Touch and hold a photo or video in Photos, tap Hide, then confirm. It moves out of your library and into Hidden.

  1. One photo: touch and hold it, tap Hide, then confirm.
  2. Several at once: tap Select, tap each photo, tap the More (…) button, then Hide.
  3. From an open photo: tap the More (…) button at the top and choose Hide.

Videos, screenshots and Live Photos hide the same way. A photo in another app (Messages, WhatsApp, Files) has to be saved to Photos first, and the copy in that app stays where it is. There's no setting that sends new camera photos straight to Hidden. For every other way to hide photos, including locked notes and vault apps, see how to hide photos on iPhone.

How do you unhide a photo?

Open the Hidden album, touch and hold the photo, and tap Unhide. It goes back to your library in its original place by date. To unhide several, tap Select in the Hidden album, choose the photos, tap the More (…) button and tap Unhide.

How do you lock hidden photos on iPhone?

They're already locked on iOS 16 and later. Check that the lock is on in Settings › Apps › Photos: Use Face ID (or Use Touch ID or Use Passcode) should be on. The same switch locks Recently Deleted.

What you can't do is give the Hidden album its own password. If you want a lock that's separate from your passcode, you have three options:

  • Lock the whole Photos app: touch and hold the Photos icon and tap Require Face ID (iOS 18 and later). It still falls back to your passcode.
  • A locked note with a separate Notes password, for a handful of photos.
  • A vault app with its own PIN, password or pattern.

Do hidden photos go to iCloud?

Yes, if iCloud Photos is on. Hidden photos upload like any other photo, and Apple says photos you hide on one device are hidden on your other devices too. If iCloud Photos is off, they're part of your iPhone's iCloud Backup instead, with the rest of your library (Apple: What does iCloud back up?).

In iCloud, hidden photos get the same protection as the rest of your library. Apple holds the encryption keys for iCloud Photos unless you turn on Advanced Data Protection, which makes Photos end-to-end encrypted. Hiding a photo doesn't add encryption of its own; read is the iPhone Hidden album actually private? for what that means in practice.

Do hidden photos transfer to a new iPhone?

Yes, with the rest of your library, as long as it transfers. How they arrive depends on iCloud Photos:

Whatever the route, open the Hidden album on the new iPhone and compare the count before you erase the old one. Photos inside vault apps are a different story: many vault apps leave their files out of the iPhone backup on purpose, so read how to move a photo vault to a new iPhone before you switch.

How do you view hidden photos on a Mac or Windows PC?

On a Mac, the Hidden album is off by default. In Photos, choose View › Show Hidden Photo Album, select Hidden in the sidebar under Utilities, click View Album and unlock with Touch ID or your login password.

On a Windows PC, or any computer, use a browser: go to icloud.com/photos, sign in, select Hidden in the sidebar, then Show Hidden Photos (Apple: Hide photos on iCloud.com). This needs iCloud Photos on. People report mixed results seeing hidden photos through the iCloud for Windows app, so iCloud.com is the reliable route.

Can you have more than one Hidden album?

No. iOS has exactly one Hidden album, and you can't create folders or albums inside it. Everything hidden sits in one grid, behind one lock. If you want separate private albums, for example one for documents and one for personal photos, see how to get multiple hidden albums on iPhone.

What doesn't the Hidden album protect?

It keeps photos out of sight, not out of reach. Anyone with your passcode can open it, it appears on every iPad and Mac signed in to your Apple Account, and it isn't separately encrypted. A hidden photo is the same file in the same library, with a flag that says "don't show this".

Questions people ask

Where is the Hidden album in iOS 26?

In Photos, tap Collections and scroll down to Utilities. Hidden is listed there with Recently Deleted. If it's missing, turn on Show Hidden Album in Settings › Apps › Photos.

Does hiding a photo delete it?

No. Hiding only moves the photo out of your library view. It stays on your iPhone and in iCloud Photos, and you can unhide it at any time.

Can someone see my hidden photos?

Anyone who knows your iPhone passcode can, and so can anyone who can unlock a Mac or iPad signed in to your Apple Account with iCloud Photos on. Someone scrolling your unlocked iPhone can't, because the album asks for Face ID.

Do hidden photos show up in other apps?

Not while the Hidden album is locked, which it is by default. If you turn off Use Face ID in Settings › Apps › Photos, apps with full access to your library can request hidden photos.

Why did my hidden photos disappear?

Most often, Show Hidden Album was turned off, or iCloud Photos hasn't finished syncing on that device. Check Settings › Apps › Photos, then look in Recently Deleted, where deleted photos stay for 30 days.
