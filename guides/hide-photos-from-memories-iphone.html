How to hide photos from Memories on iPhone

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 23 November 2026

Short answer

Hide the photo: touch and hold it, tap Hide, and it stops appearing in Memories, featured photos and widgets. To keep a person, place, day or holiday out of Memories without hiding photos, touch and hold a memory and tap Feature Less. To start over, use Reset Suggested Memories in Settings › Apps › Photos.

Memories turns your library into slideshows with music, and the Photos widget and featured photos show pictures on your Home Screen. That's lovely until a private photo, an ex, or a hard date shows up in front of someone. This guide covers every way to keep photos out of them on iOS 26, from one photo to whole people and places. For hiding in general, see how to hide photos on iPhone.

How do you stop a photo appearing in Memories?

Hide it. A hidden photo disappears from your Library, Memories, featured photos and the Photos widget, and shows only in the Hidden album.

  1. Find the photo in your Library or in the memory itself.
  2. Touch and hold it, or open it and tap the More (…) button.
  3. Tap Hide and confirm.

Memories that are already made drop the photo too. To find it again, open Collections › Utilities › Hidden; how to view hidden photos on iPhone has the details.

How do you see less of a person, place or date?

Use Feature Less on a memory. It keeps the photos in your library but tells Photos to show that person, place or day less often (Apple: Manage memories and featured photos).

  1. Open Photos and find Memories in Collections.
  2. Touch and hold a memory that features what you want to see less of.
  3. Tap Feature Less, then choose one of: Feature a Person Less, Feature This Place Less, Feature This Holiday Less, Feature This Day Less or Feature These Days Less.

To delete a memory you don't want, touch and hold it and tap Delete Memory. That deletes only the slideshow, not the photos in it.

How do you stop a person appearing at all?

Open a photo of them and choose Never Feature This Person. Apple's steps:

  1. Open a photo of the person and swipe up to see the details.
  2. Tap their face thumbnail.
  3. Choose Feature This Person Less or Never Feature This Person, then tap Confirm.

Their photos stay in your library and in search. They just stop being used in Memories and featured photos.

What can you change in Settings?

Two switches in Settings › Apps › Photos help:

  • Show Holiday Events: turn it off to stop memories built around holidays in your region.
  • Reset Suggested Memories: clears the memories Photos has suggested. Use Reset People & Pets Suggestions to undo every Feature Less and Never Feature choice for people.

The Photos widget shows featured photos on your Home Screen. If you don't want any photos showing there, touch and hold the widget and tap Remove Widget.

Where else can private photos pop up on their own?

Featured photos and Memories aren't the only places Photos picks pictures for you. Check these too:

  • Lock Screen Photo Shuffle. If your wallpaper shuffles photos, edit it from the Lock Screen and choose Select Photos Manually instead of featured photos, so only pictures you picked appear.
  • The Photos widget. It shows featured photos on your Home Screen. Hidden photos are left out, but anything else in your library can appear.
  • Your Mac and iPad. They make their own Memories from the same library. Use Feature Less there too, or hide the photo once and it's hidden on every device with iCloud Photos.
  • Shared albums and the iCloud Shared Photo Library. Photos in them are visible to everyone you share with, whatever your Memories settings.

If a photo keeps surfacing, hiding it is the one step that covers all of these at once.

What doesn't this protect?

Keeping photos out of Memories is about what pops up on its own, not about who can open them.

  • Feature Less isn't hiding. The photos are still in your Library, albums and search.
  • Hidden photos open with your passcode. Anyone who knows it can open the Hidden album; see is the iPhone Hidden album private?
  • Other devices. With iCloud Photos on, your iPad and Mac have the same photos, and their own Memories.

Questions people ask

Do hidden photos show up in Memories?

No. Hidden photos are left out of Memories, featured photos and the Photos widget.

How do I stop an ex showing up in Memories?

Open a photo of them, swipe up, tap their face and choose Never Feature This Person. Their photos stay in your library but stop appearing in Memories.

Does deleting a memory delete the photos?

No. Delete Memory removes only the slideshow. The photos stay in your library.

Do Memories on my Mac follow my iPhone settings?

Hidden photos are hidden on every device with iCloud Photos on. Photos on a Mac makes its own Memories, so use Feature Less there as well.

Can I turn off Memories completely?

Not with one switch on iPhone. You can reset suggested memories, turn off holiday events, use Feature Less, and remove the Photos widget.
