Keepsafe alternatives that don't need an account

By Inertia Projects·Published 22 October 2026·Last updated 28 November 2026

Short answer

Vaultaire, Obscura, Encamera and Noxium (which we make) all work without an account or email address, unlike Keepsafe. Vaultaire, Obscura and Noxium each sell lifetime Pro for $39.99; Keepsafe Premium is $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year (US App Store, checked 28 November 2026). Apple's Hidden album is free and needs no extra account, but anyone who knows your iPhone passcode can open it.

The Keepsafe alternatives that need no account are Vaultaire, Obscura, Encamera and Noxium, which we make. None of them asks for an email address, and three of them sell a one-time Pro plan for $39.99 on the US App Store (Vaultaire, Obscura, Noxium; checked 28 November 2026). This guide is for iPhone users who want to leave Keepsafe's email sign-in and company cloud, and want to know what each option costs and gives up before they move their photos.

Only deciding between Vaultaire and Keepsafe? See Vaultaire vs Keepsafe, compared point by point.

Why do people look for Keepsafe alternatives?

Usually because of the account, the cloud, the ads or the price. Keepsafe does a lot well, so start there.

What Keepsafe does well. It is one of the most used vaults on the iPhone, with about 387,000 US ratings averaging 4.7 (App Store, checked 28 November 2026). Its help center says files are encrypted with 256-bit AES (Keepsafe Help Center). Premium adds a fake PIN that opens a decoy vault, intruder alerts and trash recovery (Keepsafe Help Center). And because you have an account, you can sign in on a new phone with a code sent to your email.

What people want to leave behind:

  • An email account. Keepsafe sends an access code to your account email when you sign in on a new device. Its help center says that if you lose access to that email, the account and its contents can't be recovered (Keepsafe Help Center).
  • Keepsafe's own cloud. The free plan backs up your first 200 items to Keepsafe's cloud. Premium raises that to 10,000 items, and the Lifetime plan to 100,000. Premium's Space Saver compresses photos on the phone and keeps the originals in that cloud.
  • Ads. The listing says Keepsafe Basic, the free version, is ad supported. Premium removes the ads.
  • Price. Premium is $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year on the US App Store.
  • Tracking. Keepsafe's privacy label lists Usage Data under "Data Used to Track You".

None of that makes Keepsafe unsafe. It's a different model: the company holds your account and a copy of your vault.

Which Keepsafe alternatives need no account?

Vaultaire, Obscura, Encamera and Noxium. Each says in its own App Store listing that it needs no account or email. Apple's Hidden album is built into iOS and needs no extra account.

What do Keepsafe alternatives cost?

All of them are free to download with a limited free plan. For unlimited use, Vaultaire, Obscura and Noxium sell lifetime Pro for $39.99, while Keepsafe's cheapest listed plan is $23.99 a year. These are the US App Store prices on each app's page, checked 28 November 2026. Prices differ by country and change, so check your own App Store before you buy.

Is Vaultaire a good Keepsafe alternative?

Yes, if the account is what you want to leave behind. Vaultaire needs no account and backs up, encrypted, to your own iCloud, including on its free plan. Its privacy label lists no data used to track you, though it does link your email address and support messages to you if you contact the developer (App Store).

The trade-off: Vaultaire is new. It had 72 US ratings when we checked, against Keepsafe's 387,000 or so. Our Vaultaire review and Keepsafe review look at each app on its own.

Which Keepsafe alternative should you choose?

Pick by what mattered to you about leaving Keepsafe.

  • No account, encrypted iCloud backup and a decoy on the free plan: Noxium or Vaultaire. Both open with a drawn pattern. Noxium's free plan holds 150 photos and 10 videos with 1 decoy vault. Vaultaire's holds 100 photos and 10 videos across up to 3 vaults. A decoy only helps if it looks lived in; see what a decoy vault is and its limits.
  • Encryption code you can read: Obscura or Encamera. Obscura keeps the vault on the device, with no cloud of its own, and gives each vault a 24-word recovery key. Encamera can store albums on the device or in your own iCloud.
  • Nothing to install: Apple's Hidden album. It's free and locked with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode (Apple Support). Anyone who knows your passcode can open it. See is the Hidden album private?

What none of them protects. No vault, Noxium included, protects photos while they sit in your Photos library before you import them, or copies you have already sent to someone. A drawn pattern can be watched over your shoulder, like a PIN. And with no account, there is no email reset: if you forget your code, only the app's own recovery can get you back in. Noxium has you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and nobody, not us and not Apple, can reset a pattern. Our guide on forgotten vault passwords covers what to check before you trust any vault with years of photos.

How do you switch from Keepsafe without losing photos?

Export from Keepsafe back to Photos, check everything arrived at full quality, move it into the new vault, and only then delete Keepsafe's copies.

  1. If you used Space Saver, the full-size originals live in Keepsafe's cloud, not on the phone. Make sure you export the originals, not the compressed copies: open a few photos and check they're full quality before you go on.
  2. Export from Keepsafe to your Photos library, album by album. Note how many items each album has.
  3. Check the export in Photos: counts match, videos play, Live Photos move.
  4. Import into the new vault, and let it delete the originals from Photos if it offers to. Then empty Recently Deleted in Photos.
  5. Check the new vault's backup has finished.
  6. Only then delete items from Keepsafe and cancel the subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Questions people ask

What is the best Keepsafe alternative?

It depends on why you're leaving. For no account plus encrypted iCloud backup and a decoy on the free plan, look at Noxium (ours) or Vaultaire. For encryption code you can read, look at Obscura or Encamera. For nothing to install, Apple's Hidden album.

Is there a Keepsafe alternative without an account?

Yes. Vaultaire, Obscura, Encamera and Noxium all say in their App Store listings that they need no account or email address. Keepsafe signs you in with an email address.

What is Keepsafe Cloud and how much does it hold?

It's Keepsafe's own encrypted backup, stored with Keepsafe and tied to your account. Its help center says the free plan backs up your first 200 items, Premium up to 10,000 and the Lifetime plan up to 100,000.

What are the alternatives to Vaultaire?

Noxium, which we make, works the most like it: no account, a drawn pattern, AES-256-GCM and an encrypted backup to your own iCloud. Obscura and Encamera also need no account. Our Vaultaire review compares it with Noxium point by point.

Can I move my photos from Keepsafe to another vault?

Yes. Export them from Keepsafe to Photos, check they arrived at full quality, import them into the new vault, and only then delete them from Keepsafe.

If a vault has no account, how do I recover it if I forget my code?

Only through the recovery the app gives you, such as a recovery phrase or key you keep yourself. With no account there is no email reset, so set up recovery before you need it.
