Why is my photo vault taking up so much storage?

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 25 November 2026

Short answer

Because a vault keeps its own full-size copy of every photo and video on your iPhone. Moving photos in doesn't free space until you delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted. If Photos was set to Optimize iPhone Storage, the vault can even use more space than those photos did. Check it in Settings › General › iPhone Storage.

You moved a few hundred photos into a vault and your iPhone is fuller than before. That's normal, and it's usually easy to fix. This guide shows where a vault keeps its data, how to see what it uses, why it can be bigger than you expect, and how to free space without losing anything. It also covers the vault's iCloud backup, and when an iCloud upgrade is worth it.

Where does a photo vault take up space?

On your iPhone, and sometimes in your iCloud too:

  • On the iPhone. The vault's photos and videos sit inside the app's own storage. iOS counts them as that app's documents and data.
  • In your iCloud, if the vault backs up there. It counts toward your iCloud plan, next to iCloud Photos and your device backups.
  • In the developer's cloud, if the vault uses its own servers. That doesn't use your iCloud, but often needs a paid plan.

How do you see how much space a vault uses?

Look in iPhone Storage, then in iCloud.

  1. Open Settings › General › iPhone Storage and wait for the list to load.
  2. Tap the vault app. App Size is the app itself. Documents & Data is mostly your photos and videos.
  3. Open Settings › [your name] › iCloud to see your iCloud storage and what uses it.
  4. Check the vault's own settings. Many show how much they hold, and how much their backup uses.

Why is the vault bigger than the photos were?

Usually one of these:

  • The originals are still there. Importing copies a photo. Until you delete it from Photos and empty Recently Deleted, it takes space twice. Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days.
  • Photos was optimized. With Optimize iPhone Storage on, your iPhone kept small copies and left the full-size originals in iCloud. A vault that keeps full resolution downloads and stores the originals, which are much bigger.
  • Videos. They take far more space than photos. Settings › Camera › Record Video shows roughly how big a minute is at each setting.
  • ProRAW. Apple puts a ProRAW photo at about 25 MB at 12 MP and 75 MB at 48 MP.
  • The vault's own trash. Many vaults keep deleted items for a while before removing them for good.
  • Other vaults. A decoy vault or a second vault has its own photos, and they count too.

How do you free up space without losing photos?

Remove duplicates first, then the vault's trash, then move big files elsewhere. In order:

  1. Delete the originals you've already checked in the vault, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos. Our guide to moving photos into a vault app walks through it.
  2. Empty the vault's trash or Recently Deleted, if it has one.
  3. Move long videos out to a computer or an encrypted drive, if you don't need them on the phone.
  4. Use the vault's space option if it has one, and read what it does. Keepsafe's listing says its Space Saver compresses photos and saves the originals to its cloud, so the full-size copies aren't on your phone.
  5. Free space elsewhere: iPhone Storage lists the apps and recommendations that use the most.
Watch out

Don't delete the vault app to save space. Unless it backed up your photos, they go with it. Offload App keeps them, but frees only the app's own size. See does deleting a vault app delete your photos?

Does a vault backup use iCloud storage?

Yes, if it backs up to your own iCloud. It counts toward your iCloud plan like iCloud Photos does. A photo that's in both iCloud Photos and the vault's backup takes space twice, until you delete it from Photos.

Every Apple Account gets 5 GB of iCloud free. iCloud+ adds 50 GB, 200 GB, 2 TB, 6 TB or 12 TB, and you can upgrade or downgrade in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. When iCloud is full, backups stop, including the vault's.

When should you upgrade iCloud?

When your iCloud is full after you've removed duplicates. Check three things first:

  • Is it the vault's plan or iCloud? Free vault plans often cap the number of items. More iCloud won't lift that limit.
  • Is iCloud full of duplicates? Delete the originals you moved into the vault, so they leave iCloud Photos.
  • What else is there? Old device backups and large message attachments often take more than the vault.

Questions people ask

Why didn't moving photos to a vault free up space?

Because importing copies them. Delete the originals from Photos and empty Recently Deleted to free the space.

Do hidden photos take up storage?

Yes. Photos in the Hidden album are still in your library and use space on your iPhone and in iCloud Photos.

Do photo vault apps use iCloud storage?

Only those that back up to your own iCloud. Vaults that use their own cloud, or keep photos only on the phone, don't.

Why is my vault bigger than my photo library was?

Often because Photos kept small optimized copies while the vault keeps full-size originals. Videos and ProRAW photos add up fast too.
