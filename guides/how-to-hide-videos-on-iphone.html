How to hide videos on iPhone, and keep them playable

By Inertia Projects·Published 22 October 2026·Last updated 28 November 2026

Short answer

To hide a video on iPhone, open Photos, touch and hold the video, tap Hide and confirm. It moves to the Hidden album, which needs Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open, and keeps playing as normal. A locked note can't hold videos. For videos that must stay private from someone who knows your passcode, use a vault app with its own code, and check that it keeps videos at full quality.

To hide a video on iPhone, open Photos, touch and hold the video, tap Hide and confirm. It moves to the Hidden album, keeps its quality, and plays as normal. This guide is for anyone hiding videos rather than photos, because videos bring their own problems: they're big, they fill iCloud, and one popular hiding trick, the locked note, doesn't work for them at all.

How do you hide a video in the Photos app?

The same way as a photo: touch and hold, then Hide. Apple's steps cover photos and videos alike (Apple: Hide photos with the Hidden album).

  1. Open Photos and find the video.
  2. Touch and hold it, tap Hide, and confirm. For several, tap Select, choose them, tap More (…) and Hide.
  3. Leave the lock on. The Hidden album is locked by default on iOS 16 and later; its setting lives in Settings › Apps › Photos.

To find hidden videos later:

  1. Open Photos, tap Collections and scroll down to Utilities.
  2. Tap Hidden, then View Album, and unlock with Face ID or Touch ID.
  3. Can't see it? Turn on Settings › Apps › Photos › Show Hidden Album.
  4. To unhide a video, touch and hold it and tap Unhide.

What this doesn't protect: your iPhone passcode opens the Hidden album, and with iCloud Photos on, hidden videos are hidden, but present, on your other devices too. More on that in is the Hidden album private? For everything else about the album, including what happens on a new iPhone, see hidden photos on iPhone.

Can you hide a video in a locked note?

No. Apple says you can't lock notes that contain video, audio, PDFs or tags (Apple: Lock notes on iPhone). A note with a video in it stays unlocked, so it hides nothing. Locked notes work only for photos, and only a handful; the guide to hiding photos covers that method.

How do you hide videos in a vault app?

Import them from Photos into the vault, check they play, then delete the originals from Photos and Recently Deleted. Our guide to moving photos into a vault app walks through doing that without leaving copies. Before you trust a vault with videos, check four things:

  • Does it keep full quality? Some vaults compress videos, or keep originals only in their own cloud. Look for "original quality" or similar in the listing
  • Does it handle big files? 4K, HDR, slow-motion and long videos can be very large. Import one long video as a test before you move the rest.
  • Do videos play without waiting? Some encrypted vaults decrypt the whole file before playing it, which takes time and space.
  • Are videos in the free plan? Many free plans limit videos more than photos. Noxium's free plan, for example, holds 10 videos.

Why do videos cause trouble in vault apps?

Size, mostly. Anything a vault does to a video (import, encrypt, back up, restore) takes longer than with a photo, and has more chances to stop half-way.

  • Imports stop when the app is closed mid-way or the phone runs low on space.
  • Backups lag behind, especially when iCloud is nearly full, so the newest videos are the ones missing after a restore.
  • Playback stalls when the app decrypts the whole file first.
  • Formats get lost: Live Photos can lose their video part, and HDR can be flattened, if the app re-encodes.

After importing, play a few videos from the start, middle and end, and check one Live Photo still moves.

What happens to hidden videos in backups and iCloud storage?

They're backed up like any other video, and they take the same space. Hiding doesn't take a video out of iCloud or shrink it.

  • With iCloud Photos on, your photos and videos sync to iCloud, and hidden ones stay hidden on your other devices. Because they sync, they aren't part of your daily iCloud Backup (Apple: What does iCloud back up?).
  • With iCloud Photos off, iCloud Backup may include the photos and videos in your library instead.
  • With Optimize iPhone Storage on, the full-resolution video lives in iCloud and a smaller copy on the iPhone (Apple: Manage your photo and video storage). A vault needs the full video on the phone to import it, so let it download first.
  • In a vault app, it depends on the app: some keep videos only on the phone, some upload to their own cloud, and some back up to your iCloud. Noxium sends only encrypted data to your own private iCloud; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Moving videos into a vault creates a second copy until you delete the originals, so check free space first in Settings › General › iPhone Storage. If the vault backs up to iCloud, those videos count against your iCloud space too; check it in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Every Apple Account starts with 5 GB of free iCloud storage (Apple), which a few long videos can fill.

What should you clean up after hiding videos?

  • Recently Deleted: deleted originals can be recovered for 30 days (Apple). Open Collections › Utilities › Recently Deleted, unlock it, and delete them for good.
  • Messages and chat apps: a video you sent or received has its own copy in that conversation.
  • Shared albums and the Shared Library: in the iCloud Shared Photo Library, every participant can view or unhide a hidden item.
  • Your computer: check old imports on a Mac or PC.

Questions people ask

Can you hide videos on iPhone without an app?

Yes. Touch and hold the video in Photos and tap Hide. It moves to the Hidden album, which opens with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.

Can someone see my hidden videos if they know my passcode?

Yes. The Hidden album's lock accepts your iPhone passcode. To keep videos private from someone who knows it, use a vault app with its own code.

Can I lock a note with a video in it?

No. Apple doesn't let you lock notes that contain video, audio, PDFs or tags. Use the Hidden album or a vault app for videos.

Do hidden videos lose quality?

Not in the Photos app; hiding doesn't change the file. Some vault apps compress videos or keep the original only in their cloud, so check before you move large videos into one.

Do hidden videos still use iCloud storage?

Yes. Hidden videos stay in iCloud Photos and use the same space, and videos in a vault that backs up to iCloud count against your iCloud storage too.
