Does deleting a vault app delete your photos?

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 24 November 2026

Short answer

Usually, yes. When you delete an app, iOS deletes everything stored inside it, so photos kept only in a vault app are gone from the phone. They survive only if the app backed them up somewhere else, like your iCloud or its own cloud. Before you delete one, export your photos or check its backup, or use Offload App instead.

This is one of the most common ways people lose private photos: the vault app gets deleted to save space, to switch apps, or by someone else, and the photos go with it. This guide explains what iOS does when an app is deleted, which vault apps keep a copy elsewhere, and how to get rid of one safely. If it has already happened, start with photo vault lost photos.

What happens to an app's data when you delete it?

iOS removes the app and everything it stored on the iPhone. There's no Recently Deleted for apps. Photos you moved into a vault app aren't in your Photos library, so they aren't anywhere else on the phone.

Your device's iCloud Backup doesn't help afterwards either: the next backup no longer includes the deleted app, and many vault apps leave their files out of the iCloud Backup on purpose.

How do you delete a vault app without losing photos?

Get the photos out first, check them, then delete. In order:

  1. Count what's in the vault, including every album, any decoy vault you want to keep, and any second vault.
  2. Export the photos and videos to Photos or Files from inside the app. Our guide to exporting photos from a vault app covers how.
  3. Check the copies: counts match, videos play, photos open at full size.
  4. Move them somewhere private, such as another vault, if you don't want them in your Photos library.
  5. Cancel any subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions. Deleting an app doesn't cancel it.
  6. Only then delete the app.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in Photos or Files until you move them. Do it in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Can you free up space without deleting the photos?

Yes: offload the app instead of deleting it. Offloading removes the app but keeps its documents and data, and reinstalling puts it back as it was.

  1. Open Settings › General › iPhone Storage.
  2. Tap the vault app.
  3. Tap Offload App, not Delete App, and confirm.

Offloading frees only the app's own size, not the photos' space. To free that, move photos out or use the app's own storage options.

Can you stop someone else deleting the app?

Yes, with Screen Time. It's worth doing on a phone that children or others use.

  1. Open Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions and turn on Content & Privacy Restrictions.
  2. Tap App Store Purchases (called iTunes & App Store Purchases on older versions).
  3. Set Deleting Apps to Don't Allow.
  4. Set a Screen Time passcode that's different from your iPhone passcode, so others can't change it back.

To delete an app yourself later, switch Deleting Apps back to Allow first.

What if you've already deleted the app?

Reinstall it with the same Apple Account and look for a restore option. If the app had a cloud backup, your photos may come back when you sign in or unlock it. If it kept everything on the device, there's usually no way back unless you have an older computer backup or iCloud Backup made while the app was installed, and restoring one replaces what's on the phone now.

Don't sign up again with a different account, and contact the developer before you try anything that resets the app.

Questions people ask

If I delete a photo vault app, are my photos gone?

From the iPhone, yes. They survive only if the app backed them up to a cloud first, or you exported them.

Does reinstalling a vault app bring photos back?

Only if the app has a cloud backup and you sign in or unlock it the same way. Apps that keep everything on the device start empty.

Does offloading an app delete its photos?

No. Offload App keeps the app's documents and data. Reinstall it and they're back.

Does deleting the app cancel my subscription?

No. Cancel it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
