How to view iPhone hidden photos on a Windows PC

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 22 November 2026

Short answer

Use a web browser: go to icloud.com/photos, sign in with your Apple Account, click Hidden in the sidebar, then Show Hidden Photos. This needs iCloud Photos on your iPhone. The iCloud for Windows app is not a reliable way to see hidden photos, and a USB cable may show them unprotected.

There's no Photos app for Windows from Apple, so the Hidden album works differently on a PC. This guide covers the three ways people try, iCloud.com, the iCloud for Windows app and a USB cable, what each one shows, and how to keep hidden photos private on a computer other people use. For the iPhone side, see how to view hidden photos on iPhone; for a Mac, see how to view hidden photos on a Mac.

How do you view hidden photos on Windows with iCloud.com?

Open icloud.com/photos in any browser, then open the Hidden album from the sidebar. Apple's steps (Apple: Hide your photos and videos on iCloud.com):

  1. Go to icloud.com/photos in Edge, Chrome or Firefox.
  2. Sign in with the Apple Account your iPhone uses, and approve the sign-in on your iPhone if asked.
  3. Open the sidebar with the Show Sidebar button at the top left, if it's not already showing.
  4. Click Hidden, then Show Hidden Photos.

To save a copy to the PC, select the photos and click the Download button. To unhide, select a photo in Hidden, click the More button and choose Unhide; it's unhidden on your iPhone too.

Why can't you see the Hidden album on iCloud.com?

Usually because iCloud Photos is off on your iPhone, or because Advanced Data Protection blocks web access. Check both:

  • iCloud Photos is off. Without it, nothing from your library is on iCloud.com. Turn it on in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos › Sync this iPhone and let it upload.
  • Advanced Data Protection is on. Web access is off by default. Turn on Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Access iCloud Data on the Web, then approve the sign-in on your iPhone when iCloud.com asks.
  • A different Apple Account. The account on iCloud.com must be the one your iPhone syncs Photos with.

Does the iCloud for Windows app show hidden photos?

Not reliably. The iCloud for Windows app downloads your iCloud Photos into File Explorer, under iCloud Photos in the navigation pane (Apple: Download and view iCloud Photos on your Windows computer). Apple doesn't describe a Hidden album there, and people on Apple's Community forum report that everything downloaded except their hidden photos.

If you need hidden photos in File Explorer, download them from iCloud.com instead, or unhide them on the iPhone first. Unhidden photos sync like any other photo.

Do hidden photos show up when you connect an iPhone with a cable?

They can. Hiding is a setting in the Photos app, not a separate locked folder, and people on Apple's Community forum report hidden photos appearing on a Windows PC when they browse or import the iPhone's pictures.

  1. Unlock the iPhone and tap Trust when it asks about the computer.
  2. On the PC, open the Windows Photos app and choose Import, or open File Explorer › Apple iPhone › Internal Storage › DCIM.
  3. Look before you import. If hidden photos are listed, they'll be copied as ordinary files, with no lock.

With iCloud Photos on and Optimize iPhone Storage turned on, many full-size photos aren't on the phone at all, so a cable shows only part of your library.

Watch out

Photos copied to a PC are not hidden or locked there. Anyone who uses that Windows account can open them in File Explorer, and they may be included in OneDrive backups of your Pictures folder.

How do you keep hidden photos private on a shared PC?

Use iCloud.com in a private window, don't download, and sign out when you're done.

  • Use a private window (InPrivate in Edge, Incognito in Chrome), so the browser doesn't keep your session.
  • Don't tick "Trust this browser" when iCloud.com asks.
  • Don't download unless you need to, and if you do, delete the files from Downloads and empty the Recycle Bin.
  • Sign out of iCloud.com when you finish.

Remember that anyone with your Apple Account password and a code from your trusted devices can do the same. The Hidden album has no separate password; read is the iPhone Hidden album private? for what it does and doesn't protect.

Questions people ask

Can I see iPhone hidden photos on a PC?

Yes. Go to icloud.com/photos, sign in, click Hidden, then Show Hidden Photos. iCloud Photos must be on for your iPhone.

Does iCloud for Windows download hidden photos?

Not reliably. Apple doesn't describe a Hidden album in the app, and users report hidden photos missing from the download. Use iCloud.com, or unhide them first.

Do I need a password to see hidden photos on iCloud.com?

Only your Apple Account sign-in. The Hidden album on iCloud.com has no separate password; you click Show Hidden Photos.

Why does iCloud.com say my data isn't available?

Advanced Data Protection is probably on. Turn on Access iCloud Data on the Web in Settings › [your name] › iCloud on your iPhone, then approve the sign-in.
