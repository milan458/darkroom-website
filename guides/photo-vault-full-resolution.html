Does a photo vault keep full resolution on iPhone?

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 25 November 2026

Short answer

Not always. Some vault apps save a smaller copy to save space, turn HEIC into JPEG, keep only the still part of a Live Photo, or drop the date and place. You can test any vault in two minutes: import one photo, export it back to Photos, and compare its size, resolution and date with the original.

A vault is meant to keep your photos safe, not make them worse. But a few vault apps shrink what you put in, and you often notice only months later, when you zoom in or export. This guide explains how vaults lose quality, what "full resolution" means on an iPhone, and how to check yours before you delete any originals. If you're about to move a whole library, run the two-minute test below first, then follow how to move photos into a vault app.

Why do some photo vaults lose quality?

To save space. Full-size photos and videos are big, and a smaller copy makes the app look lighter on your phone. The common ways quality goes missing:

  • Compression. The vault saves a smaller, lower-quality copy of each photo. Keepsafe's App Store listing says its Space Saver "compresses photos and saves originals to the Cloud", so the full-size file lives in Keepsafe's cloud, not on your phone.
  • Format changes. HEIC photos become JPEG, and HDR or wide colour can be lost on the way.
  • Live Photos cut down. The vault keeps the still photo and drops the short video that makes it move.
  • Missing details. The date taken and the place are removed, so an exported photo lands on today's date.
  • Video re-encoding. 4K video is saved at a lower resolution or bitrate.

None of this is always bad. Some people choose a space saver on purpose. It's a problem only when you don't know it's happening and then delete the originals.

What does full resolution mean on an iPhone?

It means the vault keeps the exact file the camera made, with nothing removed. Your iPhone can make several kinds:

iPhone photo and video formats a vault should keep as they are
FormatWhat it isWhat can go missing
HEIC (High Efficiency)The iPhone's default photo formatConverted to JPEG, HDR lost
JPEG (Most Compatible)Chosen in Settings › Camera › FormatsCompressed again
Apple ProRAWPro models; about 25 MB at 12 MP and 75 MB at 48 MP, Apple saysSaved as an ordinary photo
Live PhotoA photo with a short video around itThe video part
Video (4K, HDR)Set in Settings › Camera › Record VideoLower resolution or bitrate
DetailsDate taken, place, cameraRemoved or reset to the import date

Apple's ProRAW support article gives those sizes and says ProRAW files are 10 to 12 times larger than HEIF or JPEG files. That's why a vault that quietly converts them saves so much space.

How can you tell if a vault keeps full resolution?

Run a round trip with one photo, and compare before and after. It takes two minutes:

  1. Pick a test photo in Photos: ideally a Live Photo taken outdoors, so it has a place.
  2. Note its details. Swipe up on the photo, or tap the info button. Write down the resolution (for example 24 MP and the pixel size), the file size, the format, the date and the place.
  3. Import it into the vault the way you normally would.
  4. Export it back to Photos from the vault.
  5. Compare. Open the exported copy, swipe up and check the same details. Press on it to see if the Live Photo still moves.
  6. Repeat with a video if you'll keep videos in the vault. Check its resolution and length.

Matching numbers and a moving Live Photo mean the vault kept the original. A smaller file, a lower resolution, today's date or a still Live Photo means it didn't. Our guide to exporting photos from a vault app covers the same checks for a whole library.

Does iCloud Photos affect what the vault gets?

It can slow imports down, but it shouldn't change the result. With Optimize iPhone Storage on, your iPhone keeps smaller copies, and the full-size originals stay in iCloud. When a vault imports one of those photos, iOS has to download the original first.

  • Use Wi-Fi for big imports, and keep the vault open until it finishes.
  • Check a few imported items afterwards, using the round trip above.
  • Before a big move, you can turn on Download and Keep Originals in Settings › Apps › Photos (or Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Photos), if you have the space.

How do you move photos without losing quality?

Import from Photos or Files inside the vault app, and avoid routes that compress. Some ways of sending photos shrink them:

  • Messages and chat apps often send smaller copies. Messages has a Low Quality Image Mode, and many chat apps compress by default.
  • AirDrop sends the original between your own devices.
  • The share sheet has an Options button at the top, where you can include all photo data and choose whether to include the location.
  • Screenshots of a photo are never full quality. Don't use them as a backup.

Can you get full quality back after a vault compressed it?

Only if the original still exists somewhere. A vault can't rebuild detail it threw away. Look for the original in:

  • Photos and Recently Deleted, which keeps deleted items for 30 days.
  • iCloud Photos on iCloud.com or another device signed in to the same Apple Account.
  • The vault's own cloud, if you used a space saver. Download the originals from there before you cancel or delete the app.
  • A computer backup, or the chat or email you first received the photo in.

If you find the original, import it again into a vault that keeps full resolution, and test it first.

Does full resolution use more storage?

Yes. A vault that keeps originals takes as much space as the photos did, and more than an optimized Photos library on the same iPhone. ProRAW and 4K video fill a phone fastest. Settings › General › iPhone Storage shows how much each app uses. If the vault backs up to your iCloud, it uses iCloud storage too: see do photo vault apps back up to iCloud?

Questions people ask

Do photo vault apps reduce photo quality?

Some do, to save space, and some only when you turn on a space saver. Test yours: import one photo, export it, and compare size, resolution and date.

How do I check a photo's resolution on iPhone?

Open it in Photos and swipe up, or tap the info button. You'll see the megapixels, the pixel size, the file size and the format.

Do vaults keep Live Photos?

Not all of them. Some keep only the still photo. Press on an exported Live Photo to check it still moves.

Can I get the original back after a vault compressed it?

Only from somewhere else: Photos, Recently Deleted, iCloud Photos, the vault's cloud or a backup. The vault can't restore detail it removed.
