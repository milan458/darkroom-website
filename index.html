Noxium at a glance

The facts, in one place.

Noxium 1.0 is brand new, so there are no reviews to point to yet. Here is exactly what it does, with nothing rounded up. The same list is on our About page.

What it is
An iPhone and iPad app that hides photos, videos and notes in encrypted vaults.
How you open it
Draw a pattern across at least 6 bolts on a 5×5 plate of 25 bolts. Face ID can open the main vault.
Encryption
Everything is encrypted on the device. Keys come from your pattern through Argon2id (libsodium); files are sealed with AES-256-GCM (Apple CryptoKit). Names, dates and places are encrypted too. The pattern is never stored.
Backup
An encrypted backup goes to your own private iCloud (CloudKit). On a new iPhone, draw your pattern and the vault restores. Only encrypted data leaves the device.
Forgot your pattern
You write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault. Nobody can reset a pattern: not the developer, not Apple.
Decoy vault
A second pattern opens a separate, harmless vault. The free plan includes 1.
Emergency erase (Pro)
A third pattern opens a harmless vault and erases the others.
Hiding the app
Change the app icon to a Calculator, Notes or Weather lookalike (Pro). Vault content is hidden in the app switcher and during screen recording.
Organizing
Albums, albums inside albums, and albums with their own pattern (Pro). Encrypted notes. Recently Deleted keeps items for 30 days.
Import and export
Imports HEIC, ProRAW and Live Photos. Built-in camera. "Send to Noxium" from the share sheet. Exports in full quality with original dates.
Privacy

How it works

Three simple steps.

No account to create and no forms to fill in.

  1. 1

    Choose your pattern

    Draw a pattern through at least 6 dots, twice to confirm. You can also turn on Face ID and set up a recovery phrase in case you forget.

  2. 2

    Add your photos

    Pick photos and videos from your library or Files, share them from any app, or take new ones with the built-in camera. Noxium then offers to remove the originals from your Camera Roll.

  3. 3

    Lock it

    Close the app and your vault locks. It’s backed up to your own iCloud automatically. New iPhone? Draw your pattern and everything comes back.

Privacy

Your photos are for your eyes only.

Noxium encrypts everything on your iPhone before it’s saved. Without your pattern, your photos are just scrambled data to anyone else, including us and Apple.

What Noxium protects

Your photos stay private if someone picks up your unlocked iPhone, looks through its files, or gets hold of your iCloud data. A decoy vault helps if someone asks you to open the app.

Good to know

Someone who watches you draw your pattern could copy it, so draw it out of sight. Face ID opens your main vault, so if that worries you, turn it off and use your pattern.

For the technically curious

Keys inside keys. None of them leave your iPhone unwrapped.

Your pattern never encrypts files directly. It derives an Argon2id wrapping key that unlocks your room header, which unwraps a random 256-bit vault key, which unwraps a unique 256-bit key for every individual photo, video, thumbnail, and note.

  • Argon2id (64 MiB RAM, 3 passes, 1 lane) resists GPU and ASIC cracking far better than legacy PBKDF2-SHA512 or bare PIN hashes
  • AES-256-GCM via Apple CryptoKit authenticates every file chunk, SQLite metadata column, capture timestamp, and thumbnail
  • DRK1 1 MiB chunked format binds header magic, chunk index, and final-chunk marker into GCM Additional Authenticated Data (AAD) so reordering or truncation is caught immediately
  • Portable room header + Keychain rate-limiter stores the 256-bit Argon2id salt in the room header so you can restore from CloudKit on a new iPhone using only your pattern
  • One-way public-key Share Extension lets “Send to Noxium” encrypt imports into an ephemeral Curve25519 envelope without holding your vault key
VaultCrypto · DRK1 Key Hierarchy AES-256-GCM 
// 1. Stretch the pattern or recovery phrase in memory
patternKey = Argon2id(
  secret: canonicalBoltPath,
  salt:   roomHeader.salt256, // 32-byte per-room random salt
  memory: 64_MiB, passes: 3
)

// 2. Unwrap the room's master key (1 header per room)
vaultKey   = AES_256_GCM.open(roomHeader.sealedKey, using: patternKey)
// Wrong pattern? GCM tag fails -> 0 rooms revealed

// 3. Unwrap per-frame key & stream 1 MiB chunks
fileKey    = AES_256_GCM.open(frameMeta, using: vaultKey)
chunk[i]   = AES_256_GCM.open(
  ciphertextChunk[i],
  key:   fileKey,
  nonce: baseNonce ^ counter(i),
  aad:   "DRK1" || uint32(i) || isLastChunk
)

// Change pattern? Only roomHeader.sealedKey is re-wrapped.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

01What is Noxium and how does it work?

Noxium is an iPhone and iPad app that keeps your private photos, videos and notes in a locked vault. You open it by drawing a pattern on a grid of 25 dots. Everything inside is encrypted, so without your pattern it can’t be read. You can also set up a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault.

02What if I forget my pattern?

Nobody can reset it, not us and not Apple. That’s what keeps your vault private. So we recommend setting up a recovery phrase in Settings › Recovery phrase. If you forget your pattern, tap “Use recovery phrase” on the lock screen. If you lose both, your photos can’t be recovered.

03Can you or Apple see my photos in iCloud?

No. Everything is encrypted on your iPhone before it’s backed up, so iCloud only ever stores scrambled data. Neither Apple nor we ever have your pattern.

04Do I need an account or email address?

No. Noxium works straight away with no sign-up and no email. Backups go to the iCloud you already have.

05Do you shrink my photos or Live Photos?

Never. Noxium keeps your originals at full quality, including Live Photos, RAW photos and 4K video. Dates, names and locations are kept too, and come back with your photos when you export them.

06What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign in to the same Apple Account on your new iPhone, install Noxium, and draw your pattern on the “Restore your vault” screen. Small previews appear first so you can browse right away, and full-size photos keep downloading in the background. Decoy vaults stay in iCloud until you draw their pattern.

07Do you support folders and albums?

Yes. You can make albums, put albums inside albums, and pick a cover photo and colour for each. With Noxium Pro, an album can also have its own pattern.

08Can Face ID give away my decoy vault?

No. Face ID is optional and only ever opens your main vault. Other vaults always need their own pattern, so Face ID never hints that they exist.

09What happens if my Pro subscription ends?

You can still view, play, export and back up everything already in your vault, for good. We never lock your photos behind a paywall. Only Pro features, and adding more than the free plan’s 150 photos and 10 videos, stop.

10How do I get my photos back out of Noxium?

Select as many photos as you like, or a whole album, tap Export, and choose Save to Photos or Save to Files. They keep their full quality and the date they were taken.

11Are there any ads or tracking?

No ads and no tracking. Noxium has no analytics of its own. Apple only sends us crash reports if you’ve turned that on in your iPhone’s settings, and they never include your photos.
