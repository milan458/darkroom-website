Cryptographic Architecture

Keys inside keys. None of them leave your iPhone unwrapped.

Your bolt pattern never encrypts files directly. It derives an Argon2id wrapping key that unlocks your room header, which unwraps a random 256-bit vault key, which unwraps a unique 256-bit key for every individual photo, video, thumbnail, and note.

  • Argon2id (64 MiB RAM, 3 passes, 1 lane) resists GPU and ASIC cracking far better than legacy PBKDF2-SHA512 or bare PIN hashes
  • AES-256-GCM via Apple CryptoKit authenticates every file chunk, SQLite metadata column, capture timestamp, and thumbnail
  • DRK1 1 MiB chunked format binds header magic, chunk index, and final-chunk marker into GCM Additional Authenticated Data (AAD) so reordering or truncation is caught immediately
  • Portable room header + Keychain rate-limiter stores the 256-bit Argon2id salt in the room header so you can restore from CloudKit on a new iPhone using only your bolt pattern
  • One-way public-key Share Extension lets “Send to Darkroom” encrypt imports into an ephemeral Curve25519 envelope without holding your vault key
VaultCrypto · DRK1 Key Hierarchy AES-256-GCM 
// 1. Stretch bolt pattern or passphrase in memory
patternKey = Argon2id(
  secret: canonicalBoltPath,
  salt:   roomHeader.salt256, // 32-byte per-room random salt
  memory: 64_MiB, passes: 3
)

// 2. Unwrap the room's master key (1 header per room)
vaultKey   = AES_256_GCM.open(roomHeader.sealedKey, using: patternKey)
// Wrong pattern? GCM tag fails -> 0 rooms revealed

// 3. Unwrap per-frame key & stream 1 MiB chunks
fileKey    = AES_256_GCM.open(frameMeta, using: vaultKey)
chunk[i]   = AES_256_GCM.open(
  ciphertextChunk[i],
  key:   fileKey,
  nonce: baseNonce ^ counter(i),
  aad:   "DRK1" || uint32(i) || isLastChunk
)

// Change pattern? Only roomHeader.sealedKey is re-wrapped.

What Darkroom Protects Against

Someone taking your unlocked iPhone, demanding you open the app (use a Decoy Room or the Duress Pattern), inspecting your filesystem, or obtaining a copy of your iCloud data. Without your bolt pattern or passphrase, every file and metadata record is authenticated cryptographic noise.

What It Doesn’t (Honest Threat Model)

A 6-bolt geometric pattern has less entropy than a long random passphrase, and someone who watches your fingers draw it can replay the path. Face ID opens your main room, so if someone could force your face to the phone, disable Face ID and rely on a long passphrase + Decoy/Duress rooms.

Real-World Coercion Defense

Someone takes your phone and asks you to unlock it. Now what?

Most vault apps prove you have something to hide. A locked folder list invites more pressure, and a fake “calculator” disguise fools nobody who knows the App Store. Darkroom gives you three distinct doors on the exact same 5×5 bolt plate — with zero UI clues that other doors exist.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

01What is Darkroom and how does it work?

Darkroom is a native iPhone and iPad app that locks photos, videos, and notes inside encrypted vaults opened by drawing a pattern on a 5×5 plate of 25 bolts. Your pattern is stretched in memory with Argon2id (~64 MiB, 3 passes) and a 256-bit room-header salt to unwrap your 256-bit vault key, which in turn unwraps a unique AES-256-GCM key for every file. Different patterns open different rooms with no visible room list.

02What if I forget my bolt pattern?

Nobody can reset it — not us, and not Apple. That is what makes your vault genuinely private. However, you can add a passphrase spare key in Settings › The door (which also supports VoiceOver). Your passphrase wraps the exact same vault key in a second Argon2id envelope so you always have a spare way in. If you forget both your bolt pattern and your passphrase, the encrypted data cannot be recovered.

03Can you or Apple see my photos in iCloud?

No. Every photo, video chunk, thumbnail, note, and metadata field is encrypted on your iPhone with AES-256-GCM before it is uploaded to your private CloudKit database. iCloud only ever receives opaque ciphertext and random record IDs. Neither Apple nor we ever hold your pattern or unwrapped keys.

04Do I need an account or email address?

No. Darkroom works out of the box with zero sign-up, zero email address, and zero servers operated by us. Encrypted backups automatically use your existing Apple Account’s private CloudKit storage.

05Do you compress my photos or strip ProRAW / Live Photos?

Never. Unlike vaults that recompress images by default, Darkroom always stores your exact original bytes — including HEIC, Apple ProRAW, Live Photos, and 4K video. Capture dates, original filenames, and locations are encrypted inside the vault index and restored intact when you export.

06What happens when I get a new iPhone?

Sign into the same Apple Account on your new iPhone, install Darkroom, and draw your bolt pattern on the Restore screen. Your contact sheet thumbnails appear first so you can browse immediately while full-resolution originals download in the background. Any decoy rooms remain hidden in iCloud until you draw their respective patterns.

07Do you support folders and albums?

Yes — we call them Rolls. You can create Rolls, nest Rolls inside Rolls as deep as you like, pick cover frames, and assign colour tags. With Darkroom Pro, you can also assign a separate bolt pattern to individual Rolls.

08Can Face ID expose my decoy rooms?

No. Face ID is optional and is strictly bound to your main room only. Decoy rooms and the duress pattern always require drawing their bolt pattern on the plate, so a Face ID prompt can never betray that other rooms exist.

09What happens if my Pro subscription ends?

You keep full access to view, play, export, and back up every single frame already in your vault forever. We never lock your existing photos behind a paywall. Only Pro-only features and importing new items past the 1,000-frame free tier pause.

10How do I get my photos back out of Darkroom?

Select as many frames as you want — or an entire Roll — and tap Export › Save to Photos or Save to Files. Your originals export in one batch with their full quality and original capture dates preserved.

11Are there any ads, analytics, or crash trackers?

Zero ad SDKs and zero third-party analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no PostHog, no Amplitude). Optional crash diagnostics are off unless you explicitly opt in, and never include vault keys or media content.
