Feature

Decoy vault: a second pattern that opens a harmless vault

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 16 November 2026

Short answer

In Noxium, a second pattern opens a separate, harmless vault that looks like an ordinary Noxium. Nothing on the lock screen or in Settings shows that other vaults exist. The free plan includes 1 decoy vault; Pro adds as many as you like.

Sometimes the threat isn't a hacker. It's a person standing next to you saying "open it". A decoy vault gives you something to open. Noxium, the photo vault we make, builds decoys in from the start: each vault has its own pattern, its own encryption keys and its own contents. This page covers how Noxium's decoy works, how to set one up, and what it can't do. For decoys in general, across apps, read what is a decoy vault?

How does Noxium's decoy vault work?

Every pattern you set opens exactly one vault. Your main pattern opens your real vault; a decoy pattern opens the decoy.

  1. One lock screen for all vaults. You draw on the same 5×5 plate of bolts. Noxium tries the pattern and opens whichever vault it belongs to.
  2. Separate keys. Each vault's key is made from its own pattern through Argon2id, and its files are sealed with AES-256-GCM. The decoy pattern can't open the main vault, and the main pattern can't open the decoy. See pattern lock and on-device encryption.
  3. No list of vaults. Noxium never shows how many vaults exist. A wrong pattern opens nothing and doesn't hint at what's there.
  4. It looks ordinary. The decoy is called "Noxium", like the real one. Its Settings look the same, and nothing in it says "decoy".
  5. Counts stay separate. Backup status only shows items from the vault that's open, so the decoy never gives away that a bigger vault exists.

How do you set up a decoy vault?

Open your main vault, go to Settings › Vaults › Add a decoy vault, and draw a new pattern.

  1. Open your main vault and tap Settings.
  2. Under Vaults, tap Add a decoy vault.
  3. Draw the decoy pattern across at least 6 bolts. It has to be different from every pattern you already use. Noxium confirms: "Decoy vault ready. Draw its pattern on the lock screen to open it."
  4. Lock Noxium and draw the decoy pattern on the lock screen. The decoy opens, empty.
  5. Fill it with harmless things. Screenshots, receipts, food, a few notes. A believable decoy has some photos in it.
  6. Practise both patterns so that, under pressure, you draw the one you mean.

A decoy can have its own recovery phrase too: open the decoy and use Settings › Recovery phrase, as described on the recovery phrase page.

When does a decoy vault help?

When someone knows you have Noxium and asks you to open it.

  • A partner, parent or friend who saw the app and wants to look inside.
  • Someone who takes your phone and demands that you unlock it.
  • Showing photos to someone without opening your real vault in front of them.

If you need more than a decoy, Pro adds an emergency erase pattern: drawing it opens a harmless vault and erases the others.

What doesn't a decoy vault protect?

A decoy only works if the other person believes it. It can't stop someone who knows more than you'd like.

  • Someone who watches you draw your real pattern. They can draw it too. Draw with your hand covering the screen.
  • Face ID. Face ID opens your main vault, never a decoy. If someone could hold your phone up to your face, leave Face ID off.
  • Someone who knows decoys exist. Noxium's decoy feature is public, including on this page. A person who knows may ask for "the other pattern".
  • Storage totals. Settings › General › iPhone Storage shows how much space Noxium uses in all. A decoy with 20 photos in an app using 8 GB invites questions.
  • Photos still in your camera roll. The decoy protects what's inside Noxium. Delete the originals from Photos, then empty Recently Deleted.
  • The law. Some countries require you to unlock devices for police or at a border. A decoy is not a way around a legal order, and this page isn't legal advice.

Questions people ask

What is a decoy vault in a photo vault app?

A second, harmless vault that opens with a different code or pattern. You show it instead of your real vault when someone asks you to open the app.

Can someone tell that a Noxium vault is a decoy?

Not from inside Noxium. The decoy has the same name and the same Settings, and nothing lists other vaults. They could still guess from how much storage Noxium uses, or because they know decoys exist.

Is the decoy vault encrypted too?

Yes. Each vault has its own key, made from its own pattern, and every photo, video and note in it is encrypted with AES-256-GCM.

Can I use the same pattern for two vaults?

No. Noxium refuses a pattern that already opens another vault, so each pattern always opens exactly one.

Does Face ID open the decoy?

No. Face ID only ever opens the main vault. To open the decoy, draw its pattern.
