Custom app icons: make your photo vault look ordinary

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 16 November 2026

Short answer

With Noxium Pro, you can swap the app's icon for a Calculator, Notes or Weather lookalike in Settings › App settings › Change app icon. Every icon still opens Noxium's lock screen. The name under the icon stays "Noxium"; for any name and picture, make a Home Screen shortcut with the "Open Noxium" action.

A photo vault's icon tells everyone what it is. A plain one doesn't. Noxium, the photo vault we make, offers three plain-looking icons, plus a Shortcuts action for a fully custom one. This page shows how to change the icon, what else Noxium hides on screen, and where the disguise ends. A disguise is the outer layer; the protection is the encryption and the pattern behind it.

Which icons can you choose?

Four: Noxium's own icon and three lookalikes.

The icons in Settings › App settings › Change app icon, Noxium Pro.

NoxiumNoxium's own icon (the default)

Whichever you pick, tapping it opens Noxium's lock screen, and the vault still needs your pattern. Unlike a calculator vault app, the lookalike isn't a working calculator: it changes how Noxium looks on the Home Screen, not how it opens.

How do you change Noxium's icon?

Open the vault, go to Settings › App settings › Change app icon, and tap an icon.

  1. Open your vault and tap Settings.
  2. Tap App settings, then Change app icon under Privacy. It shows a Pro badge on the free plan.
  3. Tap the icon you want. iOS shows a short message confirming that the icon changed.
  4. To switch back, come back and tap Noxium.

How do you give Noxium any name and picture?

Use the Shortcuts app. Noxium adds an "Open Noxium" action that opens the lock screen and never unlocks anything.

  1. Open the Shortcuts app and create a new shortcut.
  2. Add the "Open Noxium" action.
  3. Add it to your Home Screen with the name and picture you like, from the shortcut's share or details options.
  4. Move the real Noxium icon off your Home Screen into the App Library, so only your shortcut shows.

Noxium also adds a "Close Noxium" action that locks it at once. You can put it on the Action button on iPhone models that have one.

What else does Noxium hide on screen?

Your photos stay off the screen whenever the screen could be seen by someone else's device.

  • The app switcher. Swipe up and you see a plain panel with the word "Noxium", not your photos. Screenshots are different: no app can block them (see screenshot protection).
  • Screen recording and mirroring. While the screen is recorded, mirrored or shared, Noxium shows a notice instead of vault content.
  • Lock when face down. In Settings › App settings, turn on Lock when face down to lock Noxium when you put the phone down on its face.

What doesn't a custom icon hide?

The icon only changes the picture on your Home Screen. Everywhere iOS lists apps by name, it still says Noxium.

  • The name under the icon. iOS keeps the app's name, "Noxium", under any icon. Only a Shortcuts icon can carry a different name.
  • Settings, search and the App Library. Searching for "Noxium" finds it, and iOS lists it by name in Settings and in Settings › General › iPhone Storage.
  • Your App Store purchases, which list Noxium by its store name.
  • Someone who taps it. They see Noxium's lock screen. They still need your pattern, but they'll know it's a vault. A decoy vault gives you something to open.
  • Photos still in your camera roll. Delete the originals after importing, then empty Recently Deleted.

Questions people ask

Is there a photo vault app that hides its icon?

Several vault apps can change their icon to look like something else. Noxium Pro offers Calculator, Notes and Weather lookalikes. No app can remove its name from Settings or the App Store.

Does the Calculator icon work as a calculator?

No. In Noxium, every icon opens the lock screen, where you draw your pattern.

Can I change the name under the icon?

Not for the app itself: iOS always shows "Noxium". Make a Home Screen shortcut with the "Open Noxium" action and give it any name you like.

Will my photos show in the app switcher?

No. The app switcher shows a plain panel with the word "Noxium". This works on the free plan too.
