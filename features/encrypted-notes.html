Feature

Encrypted notes: private text next to your private photos

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 18 November 2026

Short answer

Noxium keeps notes inside the same encrypted vault as your photos. Each note's title and text are encrypted on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, opened only by your pattern, and backed up encrypted to your own iCloud. Notes are unlimited on every plan, including the free one.

Passwords for the vault's own photos, a PIN for a bank card, notes from a doctor's visit, or the story behind a photo: some text needs the same protection as the pictures it belongs with. Noxium, the photo vault we make, has notes built in. This page explains how they're protected, how to use them, and how they compare with Apple Notes. For Apple's own options, see how to lock photos on iPhone, which covers locked notes.

How are Noxium notes encrypted?

Exactly like photos. A note is an item in your vault, so it gets the same encryption, backup and lock.

  1. Encrypted on the iPhone. The note's text is sealed with AES-256-GCM with a key of its own. Its title and preview are encrypted too. See on-device encryption.
  2. Opened by your pattern. The keys are unlocked by the vault key made from your pattern through Argon2id. No pattern, no notes. See pattern lock.
  3. Saved as you type. Each save writes a new encrypted file, checks it, and only then removes the old one, so a crash mid-save can't leave a half-written note.
  4. Backed up encrypted. Notes go to your private iCloud with the rest of the vault, as encrypted data. See encrypted iCloud backup.

How do you write a note in Noxium?

Tap the add button in your vault and choose New note.

  1. Open your vault, or the album you want the note in.
  2. Tap the add button and choose New note.
  3. Type a title and the text. The note saves on its own, and shows when it was last edited.
  4. Tap Done or go back. The note sits in your vault next to your photos and videos.
  5. To find it later, search the vault by the note's title.
  6. To delete it, use the delete button in the note. It goes to Recently Deleted for 30 days first.

A note in an album with its own pattern (Pro) stays locked until that album's pattern is drawn. A note in a decoy vault only appears in that decoy.

How do Noxium notes compare with Apple Notes?

Apple Notes is a full notes app; Noxium notes are plain text kept next to private photos.

Use Apple Notes for rich notes you're happy to lock with your passcode. Use Noxium notes for short text that belongs with your private photos and should open only with your pattern.

What don't encrypted notes protect?

  • Copies elsewhere. Text you pasted from another app, or still have in Apple Notes or a chat, isn't covered.
  • The clipboard. Copying text out of a note puts it on the iPhone's clipboard, where other apps you open can read it.
  • Someone who knows your pattern. They can open your notes along with everything else.
  • Search by content. Vault search matches note titles, not the text inside, so give notes titles you'll remember.

Is it free or Pro?

Encrypted notes are unlimited on every plan. The free plan also includes 150 photos, 10 videos, 1 decoy vault, and full iCloud backup and restore. See pricing.

Questions people ask

What's an encrypted notes app for iPhone?

An app that encrypts your notes on the device with a key only you control. Noxium does this for notes inside its photo vault; Apple Notes can lock individual notes with your passcode or a Notes password.

Are Noxium notes free?

Yes. Notes are unlimited on the free plan and on Pro.

Is a note's title encrypted?

Yes. In Noxium, a note's title and preview are encrypted along with its text, so nothing about it is readable without your pattern.

Can I get a deleted note back?

For 30 days. Deleted notes go to Recently Deleted inside the vault first, like photos.
