Feature

Emergency erase: a pattern that wipes your vaults

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 16 November 2026

Short answer

Emergency erase is a separate pattern you set in Noxium Pro. If someone forces you to open the app, draw it instead of your real pattern: Noxium opens a new, empty vault and quietly erases every other vault on the iPhone. You choose at setup whether your iCloud backups are kept. It can't be undone.

Most photo vaults call this a panic button or duress mode. In Noxium, the photo vault we make, it's a pattern, so using it looks exactly like opening the app. This page explains what it erases, how to set it up safely, and when not to use it. It's a last resort, and it's built to be one: there's no undo, no recycle bin and no confirmation screen.

What happens when you draw the emergency erase pattern?

Noxium opens an empty vault that looks ordinary, and erases every other vault on the iPhone at the same moment.

  1. You draw it on the normal lock screen. It's a pattern on the same 5×5 plate, so nobody watching sees a special button or a warning.
  2. A new, empty vault opens. It looks like an ordinary Noxium, with nothing to say it was an erase pattern.
  3. Every other vault is erased. Your main vault and every decoy vault on the iPhone: their files, their records in Noxium's database, and the Face ID key for the main vault. Noxium's own tests check that no trace of the erased vaults is left in its files.
  4. Old patterns stop working. Afterwards, your main pattern opens nothing.
  5. iCloud follows your setup choice. By default your iCloud backups are kept; if you chose to, they're deleted too.

Because the vaults are encrypted, the files that were erased were also unreadable without your pattern. Erasing removes them altogether. See on-device encryption for how that works.

How do you set up emergency erase?

Open your main vault, go to Settings › Vaults › Emergency erase pattern, choose what gets erased, and draw a new pattern.

  1. Open your main vault and tap Settings. Under Vaults, tap Emergency erase pattern. It needs Pro.
  2. Choose what gets erased:
    • This iPhone only, marked recommended. Your iCloud backups stay, so you can restore later with your normal pattern.
    • This iPhone and iCloud backups. Also deletes the other vaults' iCloud backups. Nothing can bring them back.
  3. Confirm that you understand the pattern permanently deletes your other vaults, then tap Draw emergency erase pattern.
  4. Draw a pattern across at least 6 bolts that you don't use for any other vault.
  5. Don't test it. Drawing it erases your vaults for real. Practise drawing the shape somewhere else, such as on paper.

Make sure your iCloud backup is up to date in Settings › iCloud backup if you chose "This iPhone only". That backup is how you get your photos back later.

Can you get your photos back afterwards?

Only if you chose "This iPhone only" and your backup was up to date. Then your vaults are still in your iCloud, and your normal pattern restores them.

If you chose "This iPhone and iCloud backups", nothing can bring them back: not us, not Apple. That's the point of that option, and the reason Noxium shows a warning and asks you to confirm before you set it.

What doesn't emergency erase protect?

It erases what's in Noxium on this iPhone. It can't reach anything else, and it can't change what someone already knows.

  • Copies outside Noxium. Photos still in your camera roll, in Recently Deleted, in iCloud Photos, in a computer backup or in a chat app aren't touched.
  • iCloud backups, by default. With "This iPhone only", encrypted backups stay in your iCloud. They're unreadable without your pattern, but they exist.
  • An empty vault can look odd. Someone who expected to see photos may not believe an empty Noxium. A decoy vault filled with harmless photos is often the better first answer.
  • Someone who knows the feature. Noxium's emergency erase is public, including on this page.
  • Anything already copied. If someone copied files or photographed your screen before, erasing now doesn't undo that.
  • The law. In many countries, deleting data after police or border officers ask for it can be a crime in itself. Emergency erase is for coercion by people with no right to your photos, not for defying a legal order. This page is general information, not legal advice.

Questions people ask

What is a panic erase in a photo vault?

A code or pattern that deletes the vault's contents instead of opening them. In Noxium it opens an empty vault and erases every other vault on the iPhone.

Does emergency erase delete my iCloud backup?

Only if you chose "This iPhone and iCloud backups" at setup. The recommended choice, "This iPhone only", keeps your backups so you can restore later.

Does Noxium show anything when the erase pattern is drawn?

No. It opens an ordinary-looking empty vault, with no message, countdown or confirmation.

Can I undo an emergency erase?

Not on the iPhone. If your iCloud backups were kept and up to date, your normal pattern restores them; if not, the vaults are gone for good.

Should I use it at a border?

Be careful. In many countries deleting data after officers ask for it can be an offence. Read the rules for where you're going before you travel, and consider leaving sensitive photos off the phone instead.
