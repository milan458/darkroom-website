Feature

iPhone and iPad sync: one encrypted vault on both

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 17 November 2026

Short answer

With Noxium Pro, the same vault stays in step on your iPhone and iPad through your own private iCloud. Only encrypted data travels between them. To add a device, sign in to the same Apple Account, open Noxium and draw your pattern; every plan backs up, but only Pro applies changes made on another device.

A photo vault that lives on one phone is a vault you can't open on the bigger screen. Noxium, the photo vault we make, uses the same encrypted iCloud backup to keep an iPhone and an iPad in step. This page explains what syncs, how to set it up, and what stays on each device. For the backup itself, read encrypted iCloud backup.

How does Noxium sync between iPhone and iPad?

Each device encrypts changes before they leave it and sends them to your private iCloud. The other device downloads them and decrypts them with the vault key from your pattern.

  1. Encrypted on the device. Photos, videos, notes, and their names, dates and places, are encrypted before upload. See on-device encryption.
  2. Stored in your iCloud. Noxium runs no servers. The encrypted data sits in your private iCloud database (CloudKit) and counts toward your iCloud storage.
  3. Applied on the other device. With Pro, changes from another device are merged into the vault. On the free plan, each device still backs up, but changes made elsewhere aren't applied.
  4. Only the vault you open. Each vault syncs on its own. A decoy vault doesn't show anything from your main vault.

How do you set up sync on an iPad?

Install Noxium on the iPad, sign in to the same Apple Account, and restore your vault by drawing its pattern.

  1. On your iPhone, check that the backup is current in Settings › iCloud backup. Tap Back up now if items are waiting.
  2. On the iPad, sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, and install Noxium. It needs iPadOS 17 or later.
  3. Open Noxium. On Restore your vault, draw the pattern you use on your iPhone, or tap Use recovery phrase.
  4. Choose whether to download full-size photos now or later.
  5. Open your other vaults later. Noxium says: "Any other vaults stay safe in iCloud. Draw their patterns later to open them."
  6. Pro on both devices. App Store purchases follow your Apple Account, so one Pro purchase can cover both.

If Noxium is already set up on the iPad, use Settings › iCloud backup › Restore from iCloud instead. It brings a backed-up vault onto the device next to the vaults already there.

How do you save space on the iPad?

Keep full-size copies in iCloud and small previews on the device.

  • Free up space in Settings › iCloud backup removes full-size copies from the device and keeps them in iCloud.
  • Download all full-size copies brings them back, for example before you travel.
  • Wi-Fi only for videos makes large videos wait for Wi-Fi.

What doesn't sync, and what doesn't it protect?

Your vaults sync. Some settings and keys stay on each device on purpose.

  • Face ID. The Face ID key is kept in each device's Keychain and never syncs to iCloud. Turn it on separately on each device, or leave it off.
  • Other Apple Accounts. Sync only works between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. To share a vault with another person, use a shared vault.
  • Mac, Android and the web. Noxium runs on iPhone and iPad only.
  • A pattern someone has seen. Every synced device opens with the same pattern. If someone saw it, change it in Settings › Change pattern.
  • iCloud storage. Backups count toward your iCloud plan. If iCloud is full, backup pauses and Noxium says roughly how much more space it needs.

Is it free or Pro?

Encrypted iCloud backup and restore are free. iPhone and iPad sync, which keeps both devices up to date with each other, is part of Noxium Pro.

The free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes, 1 decoy vault, and full iCloud backup and restore. See pricing.

Questions people ask

Can a photo vault app sync between iPhone and iPad?

Some can. Noxium Pro syncs the same encrypted vault through your private iCloud, so both devices show the same photos, videos and notes.

Can Apple see my synced photos?

Noxium encrypts everything on the device before upload, so iCloud holds only encrypted data. Without your pattern or recovery phrase, it can't be read.

Do I need Pro on the iPad too?

Pro is tied to your Apple Account through the App Store, so a purchase made on one device can be used on the other.

What happens to sync if Pro ends?

Each device keeps backing up, and you keep full access to view and export everything already in the vault. Changes made on one device stop appearing on the other.
