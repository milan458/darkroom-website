Album patterns: lock one album with its own pattern

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 17 November 2026

Short answer

iPhone's Photos app can't lock a single album; it only locks the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums. In Noxium Pro, you can give any album its own pattern. The album stays locked inside the open vault until that pattern is drawn, and its photos are sealed with a key of their own.

Sometimes one vault isn't enough separation. You might hand your phone over with Noxium open to show holiday photos, but keep one album shut. Noxium, the photo vault we make, lets you put a second lock on a single album. This page covers how it works, how to set it up, and what it doesn't do. If you only need Apple's built-in options, see how to lock photos on iPhone with Face ID.

How does an album pattern work?

The album gets its own encryption key, and only its pattern unlocks that key. Opening the vault isn't enough.

  1. A key of its own. When you set an album pattern, Noxium makes a new random key for the album and re-encrypts the album's photos, videos and notes with it.
  2. Sealed by the pattern. That key is sealed with a key made from the album pattern through Argon2id, the same slow function that protects your vault pattern. See on-device encryption.
  3. Locked inside the vault. In the album list, a locked album shows a lock, the words "Locked album" and no cover photo, because its photos stay encrypted until you draw its pattern.
  4. Albums inside it are covered too. An album nested inside a locked album uses the nearest locked album's key.
  5. It locks again with the vault. Once you draw the album pattern, the album stays open until Noxium locks. The next time you open the vault, it's locked again.

How do you lock an album with its own pattern?

Touch and hold the album, tap Give it its own pattern, and draw a new pattern.

  1. Open your vault and go to Albums. To make a new album first, tap New album.
  2. Touch and hold the album you want to lock.
  3. Tap Give it its own pattern. On the free plan, this opens the Pro screen.
  4. Draw the album pattern across at least 6 bolts. Noxium explains: "This album stays locked inside the open vault until this pattern is drawn."
  5. To open it, tap the album and draw its pattern on the Album locked screen.
  6. To remove the pattern, open the album, then touch and hold it in the list and tap Remove album pattern.

Use a pattern you don't use for any vault, and practise it. Noxium can't reset an album pattern any more than a vault pattern.

How is this different from a decoy vault?

An album pattern hides one album inside a vault you've opened. A decoy vault is a whole separate vault with its own pattern.

  • Use an album pattern when you're happy to open Noxium in front of someone but want one album to stay shut.
  • Use a decoy vault when someone asks you to open Noxium and you don't want them to see anything real.

What doesn't an album pattern protect?

It keeps an album shut; it doesn't hide that the album exists.

  • The album is visible. Anyone in the vault sees its name and the lock, and may ask you to open it. Choose a dull name, or use a decoy vault instead.
  • Someone who watches you draw it. As with any pattern, cover the screen with your hand.
  • A forgotten album pattern. Nobody can reset it: not us, not Apple. The vault's recovery phrase opens the vault, not a locked album inside it.
  • Copies outside Noxium. Photos still in your camera roll or in Recently Deleted in Photos aren't covered.

Is it free or Pro?

Album patterns are part of Noxium Pro. Albums, albums inside albums, encrypted notes and a 30-day Recently Deleted come with every plan.

The free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes, 1 decoy vault, and full iCloud backup and restore. See pricing.

Questions people ask

Can you lock individual albums on iPhone?

Not in the Photos app. iOS locks only the Hidden and Recently Deleted albums, with Face ID or your passcode. To lock one album of your choice, you need a vault app with per-album locks.

Is a locked album encrypted separately?

Yes. In Noxium, a locked album has its own key, sealed by its pattern, so opening the vault doesn't decrypt it.

Can Face ID open a locked album?

No. Face ID can open your main vault, but a locked album always needs its own pattern.

What happens if I forget an album pattern?

The album stays locked. Nobody can reset it, and the vault's recovery phrase doesn't open it, so practise the pattern before you rely on it.
