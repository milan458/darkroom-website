Keepsafe review: how it compares with Noxium in 2026

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 14 November 2026

Short answer

Keepsafe is a polished, actively updated vault that syncs across iPhone and Android through an email account and its own Private Cloud. Its free plan is supported by ads, and Premium is a subscription. Pick Keepsafe if you want an email account to recover through and Android support; pick Noxium if you want no account, a backup encrypted in your own iCloud and a one-time price.

Keepsafe has been on the App Store since April 2012 and has 386,264 US ratings at 4.7 stars, which makes it one of the best-known photo vaults there is. This review looks at what Keepsafe does well and where it falls short, using its App Store listing (checked on 14 November 2026) and its help center, then compares it with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does Keepsafe do well, and where does it fall short?

Keepsafe is mature, well designed and works across platforms. Its trade-offs come from the account, the company cloud and the ads.

What Keepsafe does well

  • It's actively maintained. Version 13.36.0 came out on 12 November 2026.
  • Android and iPhone. The listing says your items sync to all your Keepsafe installations on any platform. If you switch between iPhone and Android, that's rare among vaults.
  • Recovery through email. Because there's an account, there's a way back in if you forget your PIN, and the help center explains what to do if you lose access to that email.
  • Useful extras. Album Lock gives single albums their own PIN (Premium), Safe Send shares a photo for a limited time, and Trash Recovery brings back items you deleted by mistake (Premium).
  • Space Saver. Premium can keep small copies on the phone and the originals in the cloud, which frees space on a full iPhone.
  • A built-in camera, albums, slideshows, and iPad support.

Where Keepsafe falls short

  • An account and a company cloud. Your vault is tied to an email address, and your backup lives on Keepsafe's servers, not in your own iCloud.
  • Vague encryption claims in the listing. "Military-grade" says little; the help center is more specific and names AES-256.
  • Ads on the free plan, and its privacy label lists data used to track you.
  • Subscription only. The listing shows monthly and yearly Premium, with no lifetime option, and the free plan backs up only your first 200 items.
  • Space Saver's catch. With it on, the full-size originals exist only in Keepsafe's cloud. Leaving later means downloading them first.

How are Keepsafe and Noxium different?

The main difference is who holds your account and your backup. With Keepsafe, the company does. With Noxium, there is no account, and the backup sits in your own iCloud, encrypted with a key only your pattern makes.

Account and recovery

Keepsafe signs you in with an email address, which gives you a reset path. Noxium has no account and no reset by anyone, including us or Apple. Instead, you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault if you forget your pattern. Lose both and nobody can open the vault, which is also why nobody else can.

Backup

Keepsafe backs up to its Private Cloud: the first 200 items on the free plan, up to 10,000 with Premium. Noxium backs up everything, free, to your own private iCloud, and only encrypted data leaves the phone. See encrypted iCloud backup for what's uploaded and how restore works.

Encryption

Both say they use AES-256. Noxium names the whole chain: Argon2id turns your pattern into a key, every file is sealed with AES-256-GCM on the iPhone, and names, dates and places are encrypted too. With Keepsafe, a copy of your files also lives on the company's servers, tied to your account.

Decoys and lock

Noxium opens with a pattern across a 5×5 plate, and a second pattern opens a separate decoy vault, 1 on the free plan. Keepsafe opens with a PIN, and its listing mentions Touch ID. Its listing doesn't mention a decoy, though its help center has an article about moving content out of a "Decoy Vault".

Price and privacy

Over a year, Keepsafe Premium is $23.99; Noxium Pro is $17.99. Noxium also sells Pro once for $39.99; Keepsafe's listing shows no lifetime plan. Noxium has no ads and no analytics, and its privacy label is Data Not Collected.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick Keepsafe if you use Android too, you want an email account to recover through, or you want Space Saver to free up phone storage.
  • Pick Noxium if you don't want an account or a company cloud, you want your backup in your own iCloud, encrypted, and you'd rather pay once than subscribe.

How do you switch from Keepsafe without losing photos?

Export from Keepsafe to your Photos library, check everything arrived at full quality, import it into the new vault, and only then delete Keepsafe's copies.

  1. Mind Space Saver. If you use it, the originals are in Keepsafe's cloud. Make sure what you export is full size: export a few photos first and check them before you go on.
  2. Export album by album. Keepsafe's help center describes it: open an album, select the items, tap the send icon at the bottom left, and choose Export to Camera Roll.
  3. Check the export in Photos: counts match, videos play, and a few photos open at full size. Exported photos return to the date they were taken, so look further back in your library.
  4. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos turned on. Then empty Recently Deleted in Photos.
  5. Check Noxium's backup in Settings › iCloud backup.
  6. Only then delete items from Keepsafe and cancel Premium in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Questions people ask

Is Keepsafe safe to use?

It's a long-running, actively updated app, and its help center says it encrypts files with AES-256. The trade-off is that your account and your cloud copy sit with Keepsafe, and the free plan shows ads. Our checklist: are photo vault apps safe?

Is Keepsafe free?

It has a free, ad-supported plan that backs up your first 200 items. Premium costs $9.99 a month or $23.99 a year on the US App Store in November 2026.

Does Keepsafe store my photos on its servers?

Its backup does. Private Cloud stores your backed-up items with Keepsafe, and with Space Saver on, the full-size originals are only there.

Does Keepsafe work on Android?

Yes. Its listing says items sync to your Keepsafe installations on any platform, through your account. Noxium is for iPhone and iPad only.

How do I get my photos out of Keepsafe?

Select them in an album, tap the send icon and choose Export to Camera Roll. If you use Space Saver, check that the exported photos are full size.
