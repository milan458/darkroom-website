Secret Photo Vault review: how it compares with Noxium

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Secret Photo Vault (Lock Photos) is an all-in-one file locker with folders, many file types, decoy accounts, Wi-Fi and USB transfer, a $6.99 one-time Pro and a Data Not Collected privacy label. It keeps everything on the device with no backup, names no encryption, and hasn't been updated since April 2025. Pick it for documents and decoy users; pick Noxium for named encryption and an encrypted iCloud backup.

Several apps are called "Secret Photo Vault". This review is about Secret Photo Vault Lock Photos, which has been on the App Store since July 2011 and has 27,396 US ratings at 4.6 stars. It isn't Keepsafe, whose App Store name also starts with "Secret Photo Vault" (see our Keepsafe review), or SPV. Everything here comes from its App Store listing, checked on 15 November 2026, compared with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does Secret Photo Vault do well, and where does it fall short?

It's a capable file manager with a lock, cheap to buy outright and private by its label. Its gaps are encryption, backup and updates.

What Secret Photo Vault does well

  • Data Not Collected. Its privacy label matches Noxium's, and the listing says your data is stored only on your device.
  • One low price. Pro is $6.99 once, with no subscription.
  • A real file manager. Folders and subfolders, hidden folders, sorting, search, and viewers for PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Pages, Keynote, Numbers, audio and video.
  • Decoy accounts. A decoy password opens a separate, harmless set of files, and you can set up several users with their own passwords.
  • Easy transfer. Wi-Fi access from a browser, USB import and export through iTunes File Sharing, and export to the photo library.
  • Old devices. It runs on iOS 12 and later, on iPhone and iPad.

Where Secret Photo Vault falls short

  • No backup. Everything stays on the phone. If it's lost, broken or reset, the vault goes with it, unless you've copied files off yourself.
  • No encryption named. The listing describes locking folders with passwords, not encrypting the files.
  • Not updated since 25 April 2025, more than 18 months before we checked.
  • Security-question reset. An optional question can reset your password. Answers to such questions are often easy to guess or find.
  • English only.

How are Secret Photo Vault and Noxium different?

Both keep your data away from their developer. The differences are encryption, backup and how you get back in.

Encryption

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. Secret Photo Vault's listing doesn't mention encryption. See how encrypted photo vaults work for the difference between locking and encrypting.

Backup

Secret Photo Vault keeps everything on the device. Noxium backs up only encrypted data to your own iCloud, free, and restores on a new iPhone when you draw your pattern; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Decoys

Both have decoys. Secret Photo Vault uses decoy accounts with their own passwords; Noxium uses a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault, and Pro adds an emergency erase pattern.

Getting back in

Secret Photo Vault offers an optional security question. Noxium has no reset by anyone; you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault on this iPhone or a new one.

Files and documents

Secret Photo Vault is built for mixed files: it opens PDFs, Office and iWork documents, plays audio, and lets you create text notes and voice recordings. Noxium is built for photos and videos, with encrypted notes alongside, and imports HEIC, ProRAW and Live Photos and exports them in full quality with original dates. If most of what you protect is documents, Secret Photo Vault's viewers are more useful; if it's photos, Noxium keeps them closer to the originals.

Price

Secret Photo Vault Pro is $6.99 once. Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $39.99 once or $17.99 a year.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick Secret Photo Vault if you mainly store documents and mixed files, want several users or decoy accounts, need it on an older iPhone, or want the lowest one-time price.
  • Pick Noxium if you want named encryption, an encrypted backup that survives a lost phone, a recovery phrase, and an app that's actively updated.

How do you switch from Secret Photo Vault without losing photos?

Export to the photo library, check the copies, import them into the new vault, and only then delete anything.

  1. Count what you have in each folder and user, including decoy accounts you want to keep.
  2. Export photos and videos to the photo library, which the listing describes. Move documents and audio to a computer with Wi-Fi or USB transfer.
  3. Check the copies in Photos: counts match, videos play, photos open at full size.
  4. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos.
  5. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  6. Only then delete the files from Secret Photo Vault.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

A vault that survives a lost phone

Noxium backs up an encrypted copy of your vault to your own iCloud. On a new iPhone, draw your pattern and it restores.

Questions people ask

Is Secret Photo Vault safe?

Its privacy label is Data Not Collected and it keeps everything on the device. Its listing doesn't mention encryption or backup, and it was last updated in April 2025. See are photo vault apps safe?

Does Secret Photo Vault back up to iCloud?

Not according to its listing, which says your data is stored only on your device. Copy files off with Wi-Fi or USB transfer if you want a backup.

Is Secret Photo Vault the same as Keepsafe or SPV?

No. Keepsafe's App Store name is "Secret Photo Vault: Keepsafe", and SPV is "SPV - Secret Photo Vault". This page is about "Secret Photo Vault Lock Photos".

How much is Secret Photo Vault Pro?

$6.99, once, on the US App Store in November 2026.

What if I forget my Secret Photo Vault password?

If you set up the optional security question, you can reset it with that. If not, don't delete the app; read our guide to a forgotten vault password first.
