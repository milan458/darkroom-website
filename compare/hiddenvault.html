HiddenVault review: how it compares with Noxium

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 21 November 2026

Short answer

HiddenVault is a photo vault, password manager and private browser in one, locked with a PIN and Face ID, with a 30-day free trial. Its listing says "zero-knowledge" but names no encryption, and describes no backup, decoy or recovery. Its privacy label includes data used to track you, and Premium is a subscription only. Pick HiddenVault for an all-in-one locker; pick Noxium for named encryption, a decoy and an encrypted iCloud backup.

HiddenVault Secret Photo Vault, by developer Kyle Allen, has been on the App Store since August 2020 and has 2,109 US ratings at 4.6 stars. It combines three things: a photo vault, a password manager and a private web browser. Everything here comes from its App Store listing, checked on 21 November 2026, compared with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does HiddenVault do well, and where does it fall short?

HiddenVault is simple and covers photos, passwords and browsing in one app. Its gaps are encryption details, backup, tracking and price.

What HiddenVault does well

  • Three tools in one. A photo vault with custom albums, a password manager with one-tap sign-in, and a private browser with bookmarks.
  • Data stays on the phone. The listing says it doesn't store, save or access your personal data, and keeps photos on your own device.
  • A long free trial. Premium starts with 30 days free, and the listing says you get a reminder before you're charged.
  • Unlimited with Premium. Photos, videos and passwords have no limit once you subscribe.
  • Older iPhones. It runs on iOS 14 and later, on iPhone and iPad.
  • A reachable developer. The listing gives the developer's own email for questions and suggestions.

Where HiddenVault falls short

  • Encryption isn't explained. "Zero-knowledge security architecture" is the only claim; no cipher, key or where encryption happens.
  • No backup described. Photos are kept on the phone, so a lost or broken phone may take the vault with it.
  • Tracking. The privacy label lists Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You, which sits oddly next to the "zero-knowledge" claim.
  • Subscription only. No one-time purchase, and the yearly price differs between the description ($34.99) and the purchase list ($44.99).
  • No decoy or recovery in the listing.

How are HiddenVault and Noxium different?

HiddenVault is a PIN-locked, all-in-one locker on one phone. Noxium is an encrypted photo vault with an encrypted backup and a decoy.

Encryption

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. HiddenVault says it uses a zero-knowledge architecture, which usually means only you hold the keys, but its listing doesn't say how. See how encrypted photo vaults work for questions to ask any developer.

Backup

HiddenVault's listing describes no backup. Noxium backs up only encrypted data to your own iCloud, free, and restores on a new iPhone when you draw your pattern; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Decoys and getting back in

HiddenVault's listing mentions neither a decoy nor a way to recover a forgotten PIN. Noxium has a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault, free, and a recovery phrase you write down at setup.

Passwords and browsing

HiddenVault is wider: it stores passwords, fills them in, and has a private browser. Noxium doesn't do either. It's built for photos and videos, with encrypted notes alongside.

Privacy

HiddenVault's privacy label lists data used to track you and data linked to you. Noxium has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Price

HiddenVault Premium is a subscription after a 30-day trial: $2.99 a week or $7.99 a month, $24.99 for 6 months, or $34.99 a year by the description ($44.99 in the purchase list, with a $19.99 "Annual Discount" also listed). Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $2.99 a week, $17.99 a year or $39.99 once.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick HiddenVault if you want photos, passwords and private browsing in one PIN-locked app, need it on iOS 14 to 16, and don't mind a subscription.
  • Pick Noxium if you want named encryption, a decoy vault, an encrypted backup that survives a lost phone, a recovery phrase, a one-time price option and no tracking.

How do you switch from HiddenVault without losing photos?

Save the photos out, check the copies, import them into the new vault, and only then delete HiddenVault.

  1. Count what you have in each HiddenVault album.
  2. Save photos and videos back to your photo library or Files from inside HiddenVault.
  3. Check the copies: counts match, videos play, photos open at full size.
  4. Move your passwords to a password manager. Noxium doesn't store them.
  5. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos. See import and export.
  6. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  7. Only then delete HiddenVault, and cancel Premium in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Saved photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Encryption you can read about

Noxium names every piece: Argon2id for your pattern, AES-256-GCM for each file, and only ciphertext in your own iCloud. No ads, no account, no tracking.

Questions people ask

Is HiddenVault safe?

It keeps photos on your phone behind a PIN and Face ID and says it can't see your data. Its listing names no encryption or backup, and its privacy label includes data used to track you. See are photo vault apps safe?

Is HiddenVault free?

Premium starts with a 30-day free trial, then needs a subscription. The listing says Premium gives unlimited photos, videos and passwords.

Does HiddenVault back up my photos?

Its listing doesn't describe a backup. Save copies elsewhere before you change or reset your phone.

How much is HiddenVault Premium?

On the US App Store in November 2026: $2.99 a week, $7.99 a month, $24.99 for 6 months, or $34.99 a year in the description and $44.99 in the purchase list.

Does HiddenVault have a decoy password?

Not according to its listing. If you need one, read what a decoy vault is and which apps have one.
