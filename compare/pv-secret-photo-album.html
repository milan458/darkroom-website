PV Secret Photo Album review: how it compares with Noxium

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 20 November 2026

Short answer

PV - Secret Photo Album is a feature-packed, actively updated vault with a fake password, a face-down Emergency Switch, a custom icon, Wi-Fi transfer, a recovery email and paid cloud backup. Its listing says data is encrypted but doesn't name how, the free version has ads, and its privacy label includes data used to track you. Pick PV for features and cloud options; pick Noxium for named encryption, no tracking and a free encrypted iCloud backup.

PV - Secret Photo Album, by developer Haibin Jiao, has been on the App Store since May 2014 and has 30,321 US ratings at 4.7 stars. It's sold in 12 languages, and its German listing is called "Foto Tresor: Bilder Verstecken". This review is about PV only. Everything here comes from its App Store listing, checked on 20 November 2026, compared with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does PV do well, and where does it fall short?

PV has more features than almost any vault app and is updated often. Its gaps are unnamed encryption, ads, tracking and backup that costs extra.

What PV does well

  • Actively updated. Version 7.7.11 came out on 17 November 2026; its release notes list a crash fix.
  • A fake password. The listing lists "Fake password protection" among its basic features.
  • Emergency Switch. Turn the phone face down and PV jumps to another app.
  • A way back in. You can set a recovery email under App Setting › Set Recovery Email.
  • Lots of tools. Slideshow, album covers, a trash folder, a photo editor, tags, notes and search, audio files, and Nearby Drop between phones.
  • Many ways to move files. Wi-Fi transfer to a computer, plus paid backup to PV's own cloud or to Dropbox, Google Drive, Box and OneDrive.
  • Cleans up after itself. It can delete photos from the public library after hiding them.

Where PV falls short

  • Encryption isn't explained. The listing mentions "encrypted data transfer" and "data encryption" but names no cipher, key or where encryption happens.
  • No free backup. The listing warns that photos are stored on the device, and deleting the app deletes them if you never backed up. Cloud Sync is for Super members, third-party cloud backup for Pro.
  • Ads and tracking. "Ad Free" is a paid feature, and the privacy label includes Data Used to Track You and Data Linked to You.
  • A recovery email. Anyone who gets into that email may be able to reset your vault password.
  • Many plans. 7 purchases, from a $3.99 monthly Pro to a $79.99 yearly 100 GB Super Member, make the right plan hard to pick.

How are PV and Noxium different?

Both have a decoy and a disguise. The differences are encryption, backup, tracking and recovery.

Encryption

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. PV says it uses data encryption but doesn't say how. See how encrypted photo vaults work for what to look for.

Backup

PV's backup is a paid extra, to its own cloud or to a third-party drive. Noxium backs up only encrypted data to your own iCloud on every plan, free, and restores on a new iPhone when you draw your pattern; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Decoys and emergencies

PV has a fake password and the face-down Emergency Switch. Noxium has a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault, free, and Pro adds an emergency erase pattern and Calculator, Notes or Weather lookalike icons.

Getting back in

PV resets a forgotten password through a recovery email. That's convenient, and it means your email account is a second key to the vault. Noxium has no reset by anyone; you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and only that phrase or your pattern opens the vault.

Privacy

PV shows ads in the free version and reports data used to track you and data linked to you. Noxium has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Price

PV Pro is $3.99 a month or $17.99 a year, a permanent membership is $41.99, and cloud storage is sold separately: $12.99 a year for 20 GB of auto backup up to $79.99 a year for a 100 GB Super Member. Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $2.99 a week, $17.99 a year or $39.99 once.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick PV if you want lots of tools, a fake password and face-down switch, Wi-Fi transfer, a recovery email, or backup to Dropbox, Google Drive, Box or OneDrive.
  • Pick Noxium if you want named encryption, no ads or tracking, a free encrypted backup in your own iCloud, and a recovery phrase instead of an email reset.

How do you switch from PV without losing photos?

Export to the photo library, check the copies, import them into the new vault, and only then delete PV.

  1. Count what you have in each album, including anything behind the fake password you want to keep.
  2. If you use PV's cloud, download everything back to the phone first.
  3. Export photos and videos to the photo library, or to a computer with Wi-Fi transfer.
  4. Check the copies: counts match, videos play, photos open at full size.
  5. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos. See import and export.
  6. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  7. Only then delete PV, and cancel any PV subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Questions people ask

Is PV Secret Photo Album safe?

It locks photos with a password, Touch ID or Face ID and says data is encrypted, but doesn't say how. Its privacy label includes data used to track you. See are photo vault apps safe?

Does PV have a fake password?

Yes. Its listing lists fake password protection. See what a decoy vault is and how to test one.

What if I forget my PV password?

The listing says to set a recovery email under App Setting › Set Recovery Email, which is used if you forget the password. Set it before you need it.

Does PV back up my photos?

Only on a paid plan. Photos are stored on the device, and the listing warns they're gone if you delete the app without a backup.

How much is PV Pro?

On the US App Store in November 2026: $3.99 a month or $17.99 a year, or $41.99 for a permanent membership. Cloud storage costs extra.
