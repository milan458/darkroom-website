Hide it Pro review: how it compares with Noxium in 2026

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Hide it Pro is a long-running, simple photo hider with albums, a fake-crash disguise, an "escape" code that makes the app look empty, one-tap USB export, and no data used to track you. Its listing doesn't name any encryption, and cloud storage and password reset are paid extras. Pick it for simplicity and easy export; pick Noxium for named encryption, a free encrypted iCloud backup and a recovery phrase.

Hide it Pro has been on the App Store since May 2012 and has 24,814 US ratings at 4.7 stars. It's a straightforward app for hiding photos and videos, with a few tricks for when someone picks up your phone. This review covers what it does well and where it falls short, from its App Store listing checked on 15 November 2026, and compares it with Noxium. What does Hide it Pro do well, and where does it fall short?

Hide it Pro keeps things simple and makes it easy to get your files back out. What its listing leaves unsaid is how the files are protected.

What Hide it Pro does well

  • Simple and long-running. More than 13 years on the App Store, rated 4.7 by nearly 25,000 people.
  • Albums you can rename and give their own thumbnail, with GIF support, a built-in viewer and player, and moving between albums.
  • Easy to get files out. Unhide back to the gallery, or export everything over USB in one tap. That makes leaving, or keeping your own copy, painless.
  • Tricks for prying eyes. A fake crash screen, automatic locking when you leave the app, and an escape code that opens the app looking empty.
  • No tracking. Its privacy label lists no data used to track you.
  • No subscription unless you want cloud storage. The app itself is free.

Where Hide it Pro falls short

  • No encryption named. The listing describes hiding photos behind a lock screen but doesn't say whether, or how, files are encrypted.
  • Backup costs extra for real space. Cloud storage is in its own cloud, and unlimited space is a monthly subscription. The listing doesn't say whether that backup is encrypted.
  • Paid password reset. Recovery is sold separately for $4.99, and the listing doesn't explain how the reset works.
  • Not updated since 11 January 2026, and the app is in English only.

How are Hide it Pro and Noxium different?

Hide it Pro is about hiding and easy export. Noxium is about encryption you can rely on and a backup only you can open.

Encryption

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. Hide it Pro's listing names no cipher. Read on-device encryption for why this matters if someone gets at the files another way.

Backup

Noxium's backup is free, encrypted on the iPhone first, and stored in your own iCloud; on a new iPhone you draw your pattern and it restores. Hide it Pro offers backup to its cloud storage, with unlimited space on a monthly plan. For a checklist before you switch phones, see moving a vault to a new iPhone.

Decoys

Hide it Pro's escape code makes the app look empty. Noxium's second pattern opens a decoy vault with photos you choose, which can look more natural than an empty app. Pro adds an emergency erase pattern.

Forgetting your code

Noxium has no reset by anyone. You write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault on this iPhone or a new one. Hide it Pro sells a password reset for $4.99.

Price and privacy

Hide it Pro's Cloud Storage is $9.99. Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $17.99 a year or $39.99 once. Both report no tracking; Noxium's label is Data Not Collected.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick Hide it Pro if you want a simple hider, easy one-tap USB export, and an escape code that shows an empty app.
  • Pick Noxium if you want named encryption, a free encrypted backup in your own iCloud, a decoy with real-looking photos, and a recovery phrase instead of a paid reset.

How do you switch from Hide it Pro without losing photos?

Unhide the photos back to your gallery, check them, import them into the new vault, and only then delete them from Hide it Pro.

  1. Count what you have in each Hide it Pro album.
  2. Unhide each album back to your phone's gallery, which the listing describes. For a computer copy, use its one-tap USB export.
  3. Check them in Photos: counts match, videos play, GIFs still move, photos open at full size.
  4. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos.
  5. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  6. Only then delete from Hide it Pro and its cloud storage, and cancel any subscription in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Unhidden photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Questions people ask

Is Hide it Pro safe?

It hides photos behind a lock screen and reports no tracking. Its listing doesn't name any encryption, so it's best against people picking up your phone, not against anyone who gets at the files another way. See are photo vault apps safe?

Is Hide it Pro free?

Yes. On the US App Store, Cloud Storage costs $9.99 and Password Reset $4.99; unlimited cloud space is a monthly subscription.

What is Hide it Pro's escape code?

A separate unlock code that opens the app looking empty, so someone who makes you open it sees nothing.

What if I forget my Hide it Pro code?

Its listing sells a Password Reset for $4.99. Don't delete the app before you've tried it, or you may lose what's inside. See forgot your vault password.

How do I get my photos out of Hide it Pro?

Unhide them back to your gallery from inside the app, or use its one-tap USB export to copy everything to a computer.
