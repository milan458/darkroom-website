Encamera review: how it compares with Noxium

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 20 November 2026

Short answer

Encamera is a private camera and photo vault that encrypts everything you add, has no account, no ads and no tracking, and has published its file and encryption code. iCloud storage and unlimited photos need Premium, and its listing describes no decoy and no recovery. Pick Encamera for open code and a private camera; pick Noxium for a decoy vault, a recovery phrase and a free encrypted iCloud backup.

Encamera, by Encamera LLC, has been on the App Store since November 2022. It's a smaller app than most vaults, with 149 US ratings at 4.5 stars, but one of the few that lets you read part of its code. Everything here comes from its App Store listing and its public EncameraCore code page, checked on 20 November 2026, compared with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does Encamera do well, and where does it fall short?

Encamera is one of the most privacy-minded vaults on the App Store, and the most open about its code. Its gaps are the free plan, decoys and recovery.

What Encamera does well

  • Encrypts everything you add. The listing says all media you import or take with its camera is encrypted.
  • Code you can check. Encamera has published EncameraCore, the part that handles files, chunking, keys and encryption, built on Swift-Sodium, a wrapper for the well-known libsodium library.
  • No account, no ads, no tracking. You don't give it an email address, and its privacy label is Data Not Linked to You.
  • A private camera. Photos and videos taken in Encamera, or from its Lock Screen widget, never reach your photo library.
  • Your own iCloud, per album. You choose album by album whether files stay on the device or go to your iCloud, with no third-party servers.
  • Live Photos and sharing in. Live Photos are supported, and the latest release lets you share from other apps straight into a hidden or new album.

Where Encamera falls short

  • iCloud is Premium. The listing lists iCloud storage, unlimited photos and unlimited albums as Premium features, so the free plan is limited and has no cloud copy.
  • No decoy in the listing. There's no fake PIN or second vault described.
  • No recovery in the listing. It doesn't say what happens if you forget your PIN.
  • Only part of the code is open. EncameraCore is the file handling and encryption; the rest of the app isn't published there.
  • Confusing plans. 7 purchases, including two monthly ($2.99 and $3.99) and two yearly ($17.99 and $19.99) options, and a "Limited Lifetime" that covers new features for only one year.

How are Encamera and Noxium different?

Both encrypt on the iPhone, have no account and keep backups in your own iCloud. The differences are what's free, decoys, recovery and open code.

Encryption and open code

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. Encamera encrypts with libsodium through Swift-Sodium, and you can read that code yourself, which Noxium doesn't offer. See how encrypted photo vaults work for what open code does and doesn't prove.

Backup

Both use your own iCloud. In Encamera, iCloud storage is a Premium feature you turn on per album. In Noxium, the encrypted backup is on every plan, free, and restores on a new iPhone when you draw your pattern; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Decoys and emergencies

Encamera's listing doesn't describe a decoy. Noxium has a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault, free, and Pro adds an emergency erase pattern.

Getting back in

Encamera's listing doesn't say how to recover a forgotten PIN. Noxium has no reset by anyone; you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault on this iPhone or a new one.

Camera

Both have a camera that saves straight into the vault. Encamera adds a Lock Screen widget for quick capture.

Price

Encamera Premium is $1.99 a week, $2.99 or $3.99 a month, $17.99 or $19.99 a year, $49.99 for Limited Lifetime or $99.99 for Lifetime. Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $2.99 a week, $17.99 a year or $39.99 once.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick Encamera if being able to read the encryption code matters most to you, you want a Lock Screen camera, and you're happy to pay for iCloud storage.
  • Pick Noxium if you want a decoy vault, a recovery phrase, and an encrypted iCloud backup on the free plan.

How do you switch from Encamera without losing photos?

Make sure every album is on the phone, export, check the copies, import them into the new vault, and only then delete anything.

  1. Count what you have in each album, including albums stored in iCloud.
  2. Download any iCloud albums to the phone, so nothing is left only in the cloud.
  3. Export photos and videos from Encamera to Photos or Files.
  4. Check the copies: counts match, videos play, Live Photos move.
  5. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos. See import and export.
  6. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  7. Only then delete the files from Encamera, and cancel Premium in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in Photos or Files until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Noxium encrypts on your iPhone and backs up only ciphertext to your own iCloud, on every plan. Draw a second pattern for a decoy vault.

Questions people ask

Is Encamera safe?

It encrypts all media, has no account, ads or tracking, and has published its encryption code. Its listing doesn't describe a decoy or a way to recover a forgotten PIN. See are photo vault apps safe?

Is Encamera open source?

Partly. Its EncameraCore code, which handles files, keys and encryption, is published on GitHub. The listing says its code is open for review.

Does Encamera back up to iCloud?

Yes, to your own iCloud, album by album, with Premium. On the free plan, files stay on the device.

What is Encamera's Limited Lifetime?

A one-time purchase ($49.99 in the US) that keeps the app's features forever but includes new features for one year only. Lifetime ($99.99) includes all future features.

Does Encamera have a decoy vault?

Not according to its listing. If you need one, read what a decoy vault is and which apps have one.
