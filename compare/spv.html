SPV Secret Photo Vault review: how it compares with Noxium

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 20 November 2026

Short answer

SPV - Secret Photo Vault is a popular, highly rated PIN vault with albums, a private browser and places for passwords and contacts. Its listing names no encryption, no decoy and no backup: everything stays on the phone and is deleted with the app. It also reports data used to track you. Pick SPV for a simple all-in-one locker; pick Noxium for named encryption, a decoy and an encrypted iCloud backup.

SPV - Secret Photo Vault, by Maple Labs, has been on the App Store since January 2019 and has 70,209 US ratings at 4.6 stars, which makes it one of the most used vault apps on iPhone. Several apps share the name "Secret Photo Vault", so this review is about SPV only, not Secret Photo Vault Lock Photos or Keepsafe. Everything here comes from SPV's App Store listing, checked on 20 November 2026, compared with Noxium. We make Noxium, so weigh what we say about our own app accordingly.

What does SPV do well, and where does it fall short?

SPV is easy, popular and covers more than photos. Its gaps are encryption, backup and tracking.

What SPV does well

  • Trusted by many people. 70,209 US ratings at 4.6 stars is one of the largest rating counts of any vault app we've checked.
  • Simple to start. One PIN, then albums for your photos and videos.
  • More than photos. It keeps passwords and contacts too, and has a browser that, the listing says, leaves no traces.
  • No server copies. The listing says SPV doesn't keep your photos or data on any server.
  • Older iPhones. It runs on iOS 15 and later, on iPhone and iPad, in English, French, German and Italian.
  • A free trial. The yearly plan starts with 7 days free.

Where SPV falls short

  • No backup. The listing says data uses the device's storage only, and deleting the app deletes your photos. A lost or broken phone takes the vault with it.
  • No encryption named. The listing talks about PIN protection, not about encrypting the files.
  • Tracking. Its privacy label lists Data Used to Track You.
  • No decoy and no recovery in the listing. It doesn't describe a fake PIN, or what happens if you forget yours.
  • Unclear pricing. All 6 in-app purchases are called "Premium", from $4.99 to $39.99, so you only see what each one is inside the app.

How are SPV and Noxium different?

SPV is a PIN screen in front of your files, kept on one phone. Noxium encrypts every file and keeps an encrypted copy in your own iCloud.

Encryption

Noxium encrypts every photo, video and note on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, with a key made from your pattern through Argon2id; names, dates and places are encrypted too. SPV's listing doesn't mention encryption. A PIN screen alone keeps out someone holding your unlocked phone, but not someone who gets at the app's files another way. See how encrypted photo vaults work.

Backup

SPV says it keeps everything on the phone and warns you to export before deleting the app. Noxium backs up only encrypted data to your own iCloud, free, and restores on a new iPhone when you draw your pattern; see encrypted iCloud backup.

Decoys and emergencies

SPV's listing doesn't describe a decoy. Noxium has a second pattern that opens a separate decoy vault, free, and Pro adds an emergency erase pattern.

Getting back in

SPV's listing doesn't say how to recover a forgotten PIN. Noxium can't reset your pattern for you; you write down a recovery phrase at setup, and it opens your vault on this iPhone or a new one.

Passwords, contacts and browsing

SPV is wider: it stores passwords and contacts and has a private browser. Noxium doesn't do any of those. It's built for photos and videos, with encrypted notes alongside, and keeps HEIC, ProRAW and Live Photos as they are. For passwords, a dedicated password manager is a better home than any photo vault.

Privacy

SPV's privacy label lists data used to track you, and data not linked to you. Noxium has no ads, no accounts and no analytics.

Price

SPV's description says Premium is a weekly, monthly or yearly subscription from $2.99, with a 7-day trial on the yearly plan; its listing shows 6 purchases, all named "Premium". Noxium's free plan includes 150 photos, 10 videos, unlimited notes and full backup; Pro is $2.99 a week, $17.99 a year or $39.99 once.

Which should you choose?

  • Pick SPV if you want a familiar, simple PIN locker for photos, passwords and contacts, need it on iOS 15 or 16, and don't need a backup.
  • Pick Noxium if you want named encryption, a decoy vault, an encrypted backup that survives a lost phone, a recovery phrase, and no tracking.

How do you switch from SPV without losing photos?

Export to the photo library, check the copies, import them into the new vault, and only then delete SPV. Never delete SPV first: its listing says that deletes your photos.

  1. Count what you have in each SPV album.
  2. Export your photos and videos to the photo library from inside SPV, as its listing describes.
  3. Check the copies in Photos: counts match, videos play, photos open at full size.
  4. Move passwords and contacts to a password manager or Contacts. Noxium doesn't store them.
  5. Import into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, then empty Recently Deleted in Photos. See import and export.
  6. Check the backup in Noxium's Settings › iCloud backup.
  7. Only then delete SPV.
Watch out

Exported photos sit unencrypted in your Photos library until the new vault imports them. Do the move in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

A vault that survives a lost phone

Noxium backs up an encrypted copy of your vault to your own iCloud. On a new iPhone, draw your pattern and it restores.

Questions people ask

Is SPV Secret Photo Vault safe?

It keeps photos behind a PIN and off its servers. Its listing doesn't mention encryption or a backup, and its privacy label lists data used to track you. See are photo vault apps safe?

Does SPV back up to iCloud?

Not according to its listing. It says photos and data stay in the device's storage and are deleted if you delete the app. Export them to Photos if you want a copy.

What happens to my photos if I delete SPV?

The listing says they're deleted with the app. Export everything to your photo library first.

How much is SPV Premium?

The description says subscriptions start from $2.99, by week, month or year. On 20 November 2026 the US listing showed 6 purchases named "Premium", from $4.99 to $39.99.

What if I forget my SPV PIN?

The listing doesn't say. Don't delete the app, because that deletes the photos; contact the developer and read our guide to a forgotten vault password.
