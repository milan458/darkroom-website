Hidden album vs a photo vault app: which do you need?

By Inertia Projects·Last updated 15 November 2026

Short answer

Apple's Hidden album is free, built in and locked with Face ID, which is enough to keep photos out of casual view. It opens with your iPhone passcode, has no decoy, and syncs to every device on your Apple Account. A photo vault app like Noxium adds a separate code, encryption with a key only you hold, and a decoy for when you're made to unlock.

Most people try the Hidden album first, and for many it's all they need. This page compares it point by point with a dedicated vault app, using Noxium, which we make, as the example. We say plainly where the Hidden album is the better choice. Apple's details come from its own support pages, checked on 15 November 2026; for a closer look at the album alone, read is the iPhone Hidden album private?

What does the Hidden album do well, and where does it fall short?

The Hidden album is the easiest private space on an iPhone. Its limits all come from one fact: it's part of your normal library, behind your normal passcode.

What the Hidden album does well

  • It's free and already there. Touch and hold a photo, then tap Hide. Nothing to install, nothing to pay.
  • Locked by default. Since iOS 16, the Hidden album and Recently Deleted need Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open.
  • You can hide the album itself. Turn off Settings › Apps › Photos › Show Hidden Album and it disappears from Collections.
  • Full quality, full Photos. Hidden photos keep their originals, edits, Live Photos and metadata, and you can edit or share them as usual.
  • Backed up with everything else through iCloud Photos, and end-to-end encrypted if you turn on Advanced Data Protection.

Where it falls short

  • Your passcode opens it. Anyone who knows or has watched your iPhone passcode can open the Hidden album. Face ID is a convenience, not a second lock.
  • No decoy. If someone makes you unlock the phone, there's nothing harmless to show instead.
  • It spreads to other devices. With iCloud Photos on, hidden photos are on every iPad and Mac signed in to your Apple Account, including a shared family iPad.
  • Apple holds the iCloud keys unless you turn on Advanced Data Protection.
  • Photos only. There's nowhere for private notes or documents.

How is a vault app different from the Hidden album?

A vault adds a separate lock and its own encryption key. That changes who can get in, not just who can see a thumbnail.

A separate code

Noxium opens with a pattern across a 5×5 plate, which has nothing to do with your iPhone passcode. Someone who knows your passcode can unlock the phone but still can't open the vault.

A key only you hold

Every photo, video and note in Noxium is encrypted on the iPhone with AES-256-GCM, using a key made from your pattern. Names, dates and places are encrypted too. The Hidden album relies on the iPhone's own encryption, which unlocks with the passcode. See on-device encryption.

What happens in iCloud

Hidden photos sit in iCloud Photos with the rest of your library. With standard iCloud protection, Apple can decrypt them; with Advanced Data Protection on, it can't. Noxium's backup is encrypted on the iPhone before upload either way, and goes to its own space in your iCloud, not to iCloud Photos.

Decoys

In Noxium, a second pattern opens a separate decoy vault that looks like an ordinary Noxium. The Hidden album has nothing like it. Our guide on decoy vaults explains when that helps.

Convenience

The Hidden album wins here. It's free, it's part of Photos, and it keeps your edits, search and sharing. A vault is one more app and one more code to remember.

Which should you use?

  • Use the Hidden album if you only want photos out of your main library and nobody around you knows your passcode. Turn on Advanced Data Protection for end-to-end encrypted iCloud Photos.
  • Use a vault app if people around you know your passcode, you share an iPad or Mac, you might be pressured to unlock your phone, or you want notes kept private too.

Many people use both: the Hidden album for things that are merely personal, a vault for things that must stay private. For the full range of options, see every way to hide photos on iPhone.

How do you move photos from the Hidden album into a vault?

Bring them back into the library, import them into the vault, and then delete them from Photos and from Recently Deleted.

  1. Open the Hidden album. In Photos, tap Collections, scroll to Utilities › Hidden, tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID.
  2. Unhide the photos. Touch and hold a photo, then tap Unhide. It goes back to your library at full quality.
  3. Import them into Noxium with Settings › App settings › Offer to delete from Photos on, so it offers to delete them from Photos after import.
  4. Empty Recently Deleted in Collections › Utilities › Recently Deleted. Until you do, the copies are still on the phone and in iCloud Photos.
  5. Check Noxium's backup in Settings › iCloud backup.
Watch out

Unhidden photos appear in your main library until you import and delete them. Do it in one sitting, on a phone only you are using.

Questions people ask

Is the Hidden album as safe as a photo vault app?

Not against someone who knows your iPhone passcode, because the passcode opens it. Against someone just scrolling your phone, it's fine. A vault adds a separate code and its own encryption.

Can someone see my Hidden album with my passcode?

Yes. The Hidden album unlocks with Face ID, Touch ID or the iPhone passcode, so anyone with the passcode can open it.

Do hidden photos sync to iCloud?

Yes, with iCloud Photos on. They stay hidden on your other devices too. They're end-to-end encrypted in iCloud only if you turn on Advanced Data Protection.

Do I need a vault app if I use Advanced Data Protection?

It depends on who you're worried about. Advanced Data Protection keeps Apple out of your iCloud Photos, but anyone with your passcode can still open the Hidden album on the phone.

Will photos I move into a vault still be in iCloud Photos?

Only until you delete them from Photos and empty Recently Deleted. After that, the only copies are in the vault and its own encrypted backup.
